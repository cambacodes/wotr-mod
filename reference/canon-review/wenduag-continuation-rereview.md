## Remaining acquisition and integration contract

The Trickster concept now begins from a real missed-romance cue and offers a concrete in-world wager based on Wenduag's power and tribe ambitions.

Wenduag can reject the wager, demand a price, or accept a challenge without the challenge itself recording intimacy or consent.

That is a credible narrative premise, but the proposal does not yet identify an implemented quest state, native actor interaction, actual skill or roll, difficulty, retry policy, cost, and failure consequence.

The author correctly leaves the check system and persistent outcome unverified, so the wager is not yet a demonstrated gameplay acquisition route or the required Trickster fate intervention.

There is also an interaction-order ambiguity to resolve before writing.

The audit says to offer the addon option after `Cue_0210`, while also requiring that the Commander first accept Wenduag's immediate answer and that she later have a new reason to reopen the subject.

The native cue has one answer entry, so implementation must show exactly how the original answer remains intact, how the player acknowledges her boundary, and when a later addon invitation occurs.

Do not present an immediate alternate answer as if it were the later earned reconsideration described in the history matrix.

The current matrix is a useful disclosure of scope, but only the active-native-romance continuation and the living, active-companion missed-romance case have a proposed ordinary entry.

Dismissal, betrayal, death, explicit rejection, and mythic closure do not yet have proven Trickster access or current-contact routes.

The audit itself calls these unverified and excludes dead or unavailable branches from the ordinary word-floor calculation.

That honesty is appropriate, but it leaves the project-wide requirement for a bespoke attainable Trickster path to every romance history unfinished.

Do not represent a route as universal until each promised entry has a current actor/contact, quest anchor, discovery path, consequential check or decision, cost, failure outcome, and voluntary invitation.

The native poison scenes may also be unavailable to a missed-romance save if their parent conditions require native romance state.

Before counting the poison-before and poison-after scenes as reachable content on the new Trickster path, bind their entry to the actual native conditions and describe an addon-owned branch for histories where the native ritual was never offered.

Do not activate native romance or finale etudes just to make those scenes available.

The audit already says not to change native poison effects or manufacture native romance flags, so this is an unresolved integration contract rather than a contradiction in its stated policy.

## Word budget and triad review

The stated arithmetic is correct: 28,600 planned ordinary segment words across fifteen allocations, 25,300 after excluding scenes 12 and 15, and 6,600 additional words across the three optional triad scenes.

Those are proposed inventory budgets, not existing text, measured reachable branch volume, or proof that the prose is meaningful and distinct.

The ledger requirement correctly addresses the measurement tool's limits, but the route cannot meet its hard 21,000-word floor until the actual integrated manuscript is counted and independently attributed.

The Vellexia and Nurah branches now contain meaningful consent principles: each woman may refuse, Wenduag cannot answer for Nurah, and the route does not infer attraction from rivalry or ToyBox configuration.

The outline still needs actual reciprocal scenes showing why each woman desires the other, rather than only the Commander or the political arrangement.

It should also specify how a woman's refusal changes the relationship while preserving her independent solo route and the Commander's other romances.

No such text exists for review yet, and this audit makes no claim that either triad is already convincing or in character.
