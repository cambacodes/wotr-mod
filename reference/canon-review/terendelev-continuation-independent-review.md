The parent join review pins the installed `RanRomance.dll` at `281239B19FF12675D2E8E48CD86B3759C38F6598A93E13F254A044C5E990DB68` and confirms the relevant terminal cue identities and distinct returned versus confined outcomes.

The recovery evidence separately confirms the distinction between native Aeon survival, conscious undead, coercive Lich service, collected remains, and a presently living body.

The audit correctly warns that the `TerendelevAlive_Dragon` etude name does not make the undead character living, and that the collected claw or a past Storyteller resurrection account does not prove a currently recoverable soul or body.

It appropriately treats the detailed courtship rituals and nonmonogamy terms as parent-mod continuity rather than universal Pathfinder canon.

Its Sevalros and Halaseliax cautions are well framed: affection or a girlfriend taunt does not establish the asserted prior spouse or sexual relationship.

Its Trickster sequence is explicitly proposed fiction and does not claim that Trickster already has a native scale-prison power.

The proposal preserves the Commander’s need to establish evidence, give Terendelev a real choice, and distinguish failed attempts from automatic success.

It also correctly separates the returned-person continuation from a new escape for a scale-confined outcome and calls for distinct reconciliation of missing-scale, destroyed-link, released-soul, and coerced histories.

## Corrections and limits

No material false claim was found in the audited text.

The audit's word target is a planning estimate, not verified content: no continuation manuscript exists, and the proposed 22,000 to 25,000 words across 14 to 17 visits have not been counted, reviewed for distinct prose, or shown to be reachable in one or more playthroughs.

The six budget rows total 22,000 words at their lower bounds and 22,000 at the listed values, while the stated target extends to 25,000; add or redistribute an explicit 3,000-word range if 25,000 is an intended actual target rather than a loose ceiling.

Before a route can be called feasible, convert the intervention outline into a branch graph tied to exact parent terminal states, and demonstrate that at least one Trickster path remains obtainable for each relevant prior history without rewriting native facts.

The audit itself already marks late-install and consumed-scale recovery as missing work, so its universal Trickster goal remains a design requirement rather than a verified result.

The projected Targona relationship is a reasonable authored opportunity grounded in a conditional parent ending, but that ending is not itself a playable mutual-courtship route or proof that either character has consented to a triad.

The audit recognizes this distinction and should retain the proposed independent scenes and individual refusals as hard implementation requirements.

The art section correctly says the native human render and parent portrait have not yet been visually reviewed, so there is no evidence here for costume fidelity, likeness quality, or an approved adult redesign.

Likewise, the reviewed helper service is explicitly unregistered and does not establish a persistent guest actor, a conversation caller, or a working restoration in Unity.

The verification ledger is strong and appropriately requires parent-patched dispatcher tests, save/load and transaction checks, ToyBox compatibility checks, and actual Unity observation before claiming success.

No route should inherit a passing status from this research audit.
