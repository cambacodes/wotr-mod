# Nurah continuation: parent and native audit

Date: 2026-09-26.
Scope: research for a substantial adult continuation, not approval of an implemented route.
Owned outputs are this report and `nurah-parent-bindings.json`.
No production code, story, shared binding manifest, export, installed mod, or save was changed.

Nurah is a strong continuation candidate because the parent already supplies a real relationship and several consequential histories.
The extension must distinguish those histories before writing her next line.
A completed quest, an earlier flirt, a released prisoner, and a current lover are four different things.
The parent also contains a concrete Pulura action-preservation problem, documented below, which must be investigated before claiming dependable end-to-end delivery.

## Length accounting

The inventory strips markup and counts Unicode words, so it is not directly comparable to the readme's advertised total.

These are aggregate source totals across alternatives, not an achievable single-playthrough reading length.

## Character facts and limits

Native Nurah is an educated adult halfling, a former enslaved secretary, a practiced liar, and an angry saboteur.
Her smiling court historian persona is partly a cover.
Her genuine resentment must not disappear because the Commander flirts cleverly.

Native common-dialogue cues `dca30cbbd88c12747a148c71355c72d9`, `90cf31b344e54db49a529e7981c5c749`, `ea0a6345a095d07488be977208fe481d`, and `e0bf813003d903c40a33d0f17f38cf89` establish Isger, her education, service to Axilar Trezbot, and the biography that brought her to Galfrey's attention.
The biography account is spoken while she maintains her cover; it should not be treated as an impartial account of Trezbot's nobility.
Cue `9d5a13aab205b6a44be552a7249a8fa2` describes forced Asmodean worship and beatings.
Her aversion to religious institutions has a specific cause, not a generic distrust the Commander can cure with reassurance.
Cues `7b048932b4c34e34e83aec1598b64de7` and `74527ed00254b6e4ea95b7259e69e02c` explicitly reject belated compassion and forgiveness.
She can recognize hypocrisy, enjoy domination over an enemy, and still refuse to serve the Commander's moral project.

Native Trickster answer `9d8458024dabdd449a6fd678f41a27f6` has `MythicRequirement=TricksterUnlocked`.
It offers to make fools of both factions.
Her reaction develops from surprise to vengeful admiration, and cue `5d1d0043e21b65b4699ff3003ab68336` promises useful lies in Drezen.
This supports a dangerous alliance and shared amusement, not automatic romantic consent.
Native alliance etude `a879a3a637a7eeb43b40677e4a8c4450` is named `NurahRecruitedByTrickster`; the parent can also reach that history through its own non-Trickster dialogue.
The name alone therefore does not prove the player's current mythic path.

After the battle, native answer `9f05ff7a6dafa8a41967ff6931723689` offers her a place in the party.
Cue `3e3e02b20d3e8da46802a0c9814fe6ba` refuses because soldiers witnessed her betrayal and may lynch her.
An extension cannot present permanent public companionship as her obvious next choice.
Undercover work or a protected, limited operation is more credible, with objections and exposure costs.
The native ritual discussion also gives her knowledge of handwriting and occult historical material, useful for an investigation without inventing a new professional identity.

Do not write another first revelation that slavery was wrong, another first encounter with voluntary enslavement, another first perfumed seduction, another first coronation, or another first meeting with Friedhelm.
Those are existing earned events.
New consequences can refer to them when the actual corresponding answers or cues were earned.

The parent Good route permits sincere feeling and selected compassionate acts, but does not make Nurah a gentle conventional courtier.
The Chaos route prizes disruption and entertainment.
The Evil route includes exploiting others and seeking power.
Those parent developments are authored extensions of native Nurah, not vanilla canon facts.
An expansion should preserve the selected development without declaring Good the only legitimate romance.

It builds a replacement action list containing `val4.Actions.Actions[0]` twice, followed by `StartDialog(Book4)`.

Action zero unlocks flag `04841f0b76b25d84c9ee6536bd8ecd27`.

The mismatch is reproducible from fresh installed-DLL decompilation and native archive data.

## Proposed continuation opportunities

The following are authored proposals, not claims that these quests or world changes already exist in the native game.

### A disputed biography and a profitable forgery

A patron obtains evidence that threatens Nurah's published account of Trezbot and wants a revised edition that makes a slaver respectable.
Nurah wants control of the account and a useful target; the Commander may want a supply contract, intelligence, revenge, or protection of living witnesses.
Give her her own copy of the evidence and a counteroffer, so the player cannot win solely by holding the documents.
Knowledge World can expose a false chronology; a second route can buy access or exploit the patron's greed.
Failure should change who knows the scheme and the price of proceeding, not manufacture a confession.
Good play may sacrifice a useful contract to protect witnesses.
Evil play may sell a damaging version, blackmail the patron, or let Nurah frame a deserving or undeserving rival, with later consequences.
She should resist being ordered to produce a sentimental redemption memoir.
Attraction develops through competent collaboration and a private risk she actually chooses.

### A surviving witness to the old bargain

Build on the exact Wintersun, Blackwater, or Friedhelm branch rather than repeating its lesson.
For example, someone wants payment or protection because the couple's earlier deception exposed them.
Good Nurah may want one useful person saved while refusing a public admission of guilt.
Chaos Nurah may defend a successful former slave's autonomy even when his methods embarrass the Commander.
Evil Nurah may view the witness as an asset and object when the Commander gives that asset away.
The Commander can bargain, redirect the threat, accept collateral harm, or sever the operation.
This permits disagreement without requiring the player to rehabilitate her.
Do not claim to alter native Blackwater control, kill a native NPC, or free a native settlement unless the eventual implementation supplies those exact actions and verifies their consequences.

### Trickster's contested appointment

The existing Queen route already changes a supposed royal relationship through reality-bending absurdity.
A fresh Trickster intervention can exploit forged schedules, contradictory titles, or a disputed legal identity to create a meeting the authorities cannot consistently forbid.
Nurah should supply the lie and test whether the Commander understands who will pay for it.
The reward is an attainable opportunity, not a compulsory romance flag.
Already allied lovers need a continuation, missed allies need a new dangerous bargain, and hostile released Nurah needs concrete reasons to cooperate.
The king-gone branch must not quietly assert her parent coronation occurred.
Executed, Camellia-killed, or destroyed-body histories need a separately reviewed return contract and current actor proof before this can become an implemented all-histories promise.
Trickster is the required bespoke path, but the missing recovery mechanics remain work to do.

### An undercover guest operation

Her native party refusal gives this a strong conflict: the Commander wants her skill, while appearing among crusaders makes her vulnerable.
A short operation with disguise, cover documents, an escape plan, and the possibility that she abandons the Commander is more credible than permanent gratitude-driven recruitment.
She can reject the plan when the protection is worthless.
Success may earn another visit or greater operational trust; it does not automatically earn intimacy.
A book-event implementation can deliver decisions and consequences before any full companion system exists, provided it is honestly described as a narrated operation.
Physical guest travel must receive separate native lifecycle tests.

## Adult romance, other partners, and art

The parent already has explicit dialogue permitting other lovers in Book4Page002.
Preserve that characterization and ToyBox Free Love/no-jealousy compatibility rather than adding compulsory exclusivity tests.
Other romances should retain their own flags and outcomes while Nurah's choices run.
No-jealousy does not erase ideological conflict, distrust, exploitation, or a partner's refusal to join a scheme.

The parent mentions Galfrey and Nocticula and includes ending rumors or rivalry involving other lovers.
These are not sufficient evidence of mutual attraction for a new Commander-and-two-women route.
A Nurah/Galfrey proposal especially needs Galfrey's independent motives and her response to a saboteur who hates the crusade's respectable face.
It should remain optional and experimental until the third participant's own arc earns interest.
A Nurah/Nocticula proposal needs a credible reason for Nocticula to engage beyond Nurah's fascination with power.
Do not inherit the parent's humiliating Wenduag outcome as a universally desired no-jealousy agreement.

Keep mature attraction specific: strategic teasing, testing whether the Commander saw through her lie, private daring, and chosen physical closeness.
Avoid both graphic sex and generic speeches about healthy relationships.
A cruel or self-interested lover can remain cruel or self-interested.
The Commander may be good, evil, calculating, or affectionate without the dialogue pretending every choice pleases her.

For art, use the installed portrait and model as actual references before generating variants.
Retain an unmistakably adult halfling, recognizable facial structure, hair and costume cues, short stature, and an intelligent, potentially malicious expression.
Conventional attractiveness should not turn her into a generic tall human or infantilize her proportions.
The audit did not generate or visually approve new art.
Possible settings are a concealed tavern alcove, a working archive with altered proofs, or the aftermath of an undercover assignment, each tied to a written scene.

## Required verification and present limits

Before text approval, write complete alternative joins for Good/Chaos/Evil, crowned/uncrowned, accepted parent romance, prior decline, Lich arrangement, and relevant native hostile histories.
Review these as assembled paths with skeptical canon, narrative, adult-romance, and art reviewers.
Do not promise a score in advance.

Before engine approval, verify the parent binding types and source provenance independently, then test actual available/seen/selected observations.
Adversarial cases must include Romance started but no accepted terminal, Romance completed by conversion, quest completed platonically, stale finale history after death, malformed simultaneous personalities, prison, Camellia killing, and a foreign actor with the same blueprint.
Keep current-vs-Aeon scope explicit.
Play through skill success, failure, abort, resume, postponement, refusal, and late interruption without duplicated rewards or rerolls that erase consequences.
Check every supposed native-world effect against an actual native action or mark it as authored narrative state.

For physical delivery, test hidden and absent actors, native claims, chapter transitions, action failure, save/reload, cleanup, and explicit retry after partial mutation.
For ending work, test all original selectors and the optional RanEpilogue sequence, preserve page actions exactly once, and prove an applicable replacement is available or already earned before suppressing a relationship cue.
Retain untouched parent-only saves.
Test with another active romance and ToyBox settings enabled; a permissive text response alone is not a compatibility test.

This audit establishes a source-grounded design and binding starting point.
It does not establish a new complete Nurah route, a safe resurrection implementation, a working guest companion, successful Unity placement, or in-game readiness.
