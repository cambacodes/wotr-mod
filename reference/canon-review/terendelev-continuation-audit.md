## Proposed Trickster intervention, not existing native magic

The first authored delivery should extend the proven returned romantic finale.
It needs a separately accepted visit and confirmed living contact, but no second resurrection ritual.
The bespoke escape can then address the still-confined accepted romance, with a friendship version kept separate.

1. Establish the actual surviving channel.
Read current possession of the mod scale, the completed confined ending, prior refusal, and the native Ravener outcome.
An inspection visit discovers that the copied city uses remembered landmarks while the scale carries a real-world identity and location.
This is an authored hypothesis to test, not a known Trickster ability.
2. Obtain independent evidence from the real world.
Use the claw or previously witnessed Storyteller account for one memory and an accessible living witness or surviving civic record for another.
Do not demand an unavailable Halaseliax meeting as the only path for a Trickster who never earned his cooperation.
The already established prologue wound supplies an additional personal connection; it is not proof that healing creates ownership.
3. Test the false boundary with a deliberate contradiction.
The Commander arranges for a real landmark's present description to be carried through the scale while Terendelev reconstructs its old remembered position.
Knowledge Arcana can identify which boundary is copying the memory; World knowledge or actual witnessed civic details can expose a mismatch without that roll.
A failed attempt changes the next available approach and requires an additional evidence visit, rather than silently passing or permanently making the universal route impossible.
4. Resolve the divided identity before promising escape.
If the Ravener still exists, the copied-city trick cannot create a second complete Terendelev while its other part remains outside.
Provide an attainable staged return to the actual encounter or a specifically implemented late resolution if the map has become inaccessible.
Respect the native Iz death producer, rewards, Galfrey consequences, and already consumed remains.
The Trickster can turn the prison's attachment to one scale against its boundary, but cannot simply declare the missing soul half irrelevant.
5. Let the recovered person choose the next act.
She should test the Commander's proposed solution, reject a binding disguised as rescue, and decide whether the new risk is acceptable.
A previous romantic refusal remains a refusal unless a separately played later reconciliation earns a new invitation.
6. Commit the materialization only after the implemented prerequisites and choice.
Use one saved provenance and the reviewed service's stable entity transaction, then wait for confirmed contact.
An obstructed destination or ambiguous submission gives pending/failed status, not a successful reunion paragraph.
7. Play aftermath that the trick cannot erase.
The real city has changed, her former memories are incomplete or painful, and keeping a chosen promise matters more than proving the Commander can rewrite everything.
Her eventual attraction to the Trickster can center on precise preparation and the use of power to protect others despite her dislike of indiscriminate chaos.

This design is only attainable after its fallback investigations, costs, encounter resolution, and service caller exist and pass tests.
It does not yet fulfill the universal Trickster constraint.
Missing or consumed scale histories need an additive Storyteller/claw investigation with a genuinely created replacement contact object, not an ItemsEnough check against an object the game never grants.
Destroyed connection, soul release, and former Lich coercion need different reconciliation chapters with real current soul evidence.
No source inspected proves a departed petitioner can be recalled by renaming a scale or flipping an etude.
