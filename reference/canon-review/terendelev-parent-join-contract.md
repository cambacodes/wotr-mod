The existing RanRomance restoration and scale-confined finales remain the source of truth for their own outcomes.
The new delivery service must not turn the oath, romance flag or shared quest completion into proof of a returned living person.
This document specifies the read-only join needed for the next authored continuation; it is not runtime registration or a completed route.

## Relationship and native-history distinctions

The returned finale selects different cues using current romance, Aeon-scale history and Gold Dragon class.
Its conclusion therefore does not imply active courtship for every player.
The scale-confined ending can complete the romance etude in two branches while leaving the person confined.
A later opening must retain that prior decision instead of silently restarting affection or treating confinement as consent.
Any new romantic invitation needs a newly chosen response, and friendship continuation must not claim an existing romance.

Read native Aeon survival, conscious-undead and Lich-service outcomes alongside the parent history before authorizing physical delivery.
The delivery service's loaded-actor conflict check is an additional safeguard, not a replacement for those story checks when a conflicting actor is in another area.
Preserve the parent's scale dispatcher and Iz remains actions; no action-list replacement or quest reset is needed to read a completed ending.
Missing-scale, consumed-remains and explicitly released-soul histories still need separate attainable Trickster investigations and cannot be inferred from these two parent finales.
