# Kiana and Aranka, first shared rehearsal

This is new authored dialogue, not native or existing RanRomance content.
It is not exported to the playable story or counted toward delivered route volume.
Attachment is pending the Aranka contact audit in `reference/canon-review/aranka-integration.md`.

## Arrival

Narrator: Kiana has arranged three chairs in her borrowed castle.
One faces the others, as if she has been deciding whether this is a rehearsal or an interrogation.
Aranka stands beside the table, examining the paper moon.

Aranka: "A convincing theft. I haven't seen the real one all afternoon."

Kiana: "I was told you might know what to do with a song that has too many words."

Aranka: "Usually I sing fewer of them."

Kiana: "I was afraid you would say that."

Narrator: Kiana offers her the page.
The first line reads, "No road will ever lead you from my door, where all the lonely stars must wait forevermore."
Aranka reads it once, then hums a phrase under her breath.
She taps the page where the tune stops.

Aranka: "There. She has to breathe somewhere. Unless being a vampire excuses her."

Kiana: "It excuses quite a lot. But not in front of an audience."

- "We could ask the audience to hold its breath too." Continue to interruption.
- "Show us where you would change it." Continue to demonstration.
- Sit down and let them work. Continue to demonstration with silent observation.

## Interruption

Aranka: "Only if you demonstrate for the whole first verse."

Narrator: She gives you an expectant look, then relents before the experiment can begin.

Aranka: "No, don't. Kiana has enough to worry about without explaining what happened to her guest."

Kiana: "I would blame the staircase. It already has a dreadful reputation."

Continue to demonstration.

## Demonstration

Narrator: Aranka sings the first two lines, borrowing Kiana's grand manner for the princess.
The melody lifts at the threat, and suddenly it sounds like an invitation.
Kiana laughs, then shakes her head.

Kiana: "That's lovely. It is also completely wrong."

Aranka: "Wrong is less expensive to repair before we teach it to anyone. What did you want?"

Kiana: "She wants the guest to stay. She would sooner pull down the castle than ask."

Aranka: "Then why give her such a good song to hide behind?"

Narrator: Kiana opens her mouth, stops, and looks at the page again.

Kiana: "Because I wanted to sing it."

Aranka: "That is a much better answer. We can work with that."

Narrator: She covers the second half of the line with a finger.

Aranka: "The night has closed my door. Why would you leave?"

Kiana: "I've lost all my stars."

Aranka: "Save them for the guest. Let someone else have a good line."

Narrator: Kiana takes the page, silently tries both versions, and reluctantly crosses out the stars.

Kiana: "The guest had better appreciate them."

Narrator: She tries the phrase lower, leaving the last few words almost spoken.
Kiana follows her, misses the turn in the melody, and swears at the page.

Aranka: "Again. You found it just after you decided you hadn't."

Kiana: "You sound very sure."

Aranka: "I was listening."

## The part that stays

Narrator: They try it twice more.
On the second attempt Aranka stops singing, and Kiana reaches the end alone.
She looks up sharply when she notices.

Kiana: "You left me to do it."

Aranka: "You were doing it. I wanted to hear."

Narrator: Kiana glances toward you, caught between asking for a verdict and pretending she needs none.

Commander choices:

- "I believed she wanted the guest to stay." Continue to close.
- "The quiet entrance would fit before that." Available only after Kiana chose the quiet entrance in stagecraft. Continue to quiet callback.
- "Keep the ruined castle. Now she has something worth hiding in it." Available only after Kiana chose the comic entrance in stagecraft. Continue to comic callback.

Quiet callback:

Kiana: "Yes. And no music until she turns away from the door."

Aranka: "Make me wait for the cue, then. I want to see this entrance."

Continue to close.

Comic callback:

Kiana: "The shelf stays. I refuse to dismiss my most reliable performer."

Aranka: "Does the shelf sing?"

Kiana: "Only when the damp gets into it."

Continue to close.

## Close

Narrator: Kiana begins to explain the entrance.
Aranka moves her chair to see the hanging cloth.
With the quiet entrance, they debate whether the first note should come before or after Kiana turns from the door.
With the comic entrance, they debate how long the princess can struggle with her sleeve before the audience laughs.
With the unqualified compliment, Aranka asks to see Kiana's entrance once before suggesting accompaniment.
Neither asks you to settle it.

Narrator: When the light begins to fail, Kiana brings a candle to the table.
Aranka has put down her bag twice without quite managing to leave.

Kiana: "You can take the page. If you want to try anything before we meet again."

Aranka: "There is a 'we meet again,' then?"

Kiana: "There is if you can bear another rehearsal."

Aranka: "I can bear a better second verse. Bring me one."

Narrator: Kiana holds the page a moment longer before handing it over.
She is smiling when she turns back to the table.

Commander choices:

- Offer to help prepare the room for their next rehearsal.
- Let them arrange their next meeting themselves.

Both choices record the shared meeting only.
Neither starts a romance, changes existing partners or grants a triad commitment.
Their separate later conversations must establish whether this interest becomes attraction and whether either wants the Commander involved.
