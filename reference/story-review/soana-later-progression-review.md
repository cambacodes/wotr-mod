## What works

Soana remains capable of hurting others for the forest.
Her claim on Meret is uncomfortable because she really did protect the family, yet now treats inherited gratitude as a claim on another person's service.
Her eventual participation costs her something she values.
The story allows her to revise one agreement without declaring her entire worldview cured.
The explicit return to bound or dead Orso prevents the successful rite from quietly absolving her earlier conduct.

The voice route's strongest moment is the Commander deciding whether to honor her clear continuing signal while hearing the cost.
Stopping remains possible, but the nursery dies and Soana says she would choose a different partner for that practical role next time.
Her continued attraction does not erase the disagreement.
The ridge route also preserves anger after an agreed loss, giving consent consequences beyond a single affirmative line.

The later path dispute carries the forest conflict into daily life.
Protecting the deer hollow costs travelers effort; the flood route risks disturbance and requires practical limits.
Neither route grants a restored forest or makes the earlier bear outcome disappear.
Lore Religion DC 24 has a slower failed and non-roll alternative, so discovery rewards competence without making romance depend on winning one roll.

The Corven conversation makes a credible authored departure from indefinite waiting.
The final wording about refusing to put an answer in his mouth and remembering his flowers sounds more personal than the earlier statement about conduct and consent.
Soana chooses what she will do while acknowledging the missing answer.
The player can choose friendship because that uncertainty matters to them.
Other loves are explicitly possible, and the scenes introduce no exclusivity or jealousy requirement.

The last evening contains recognizable desire without graphic description.
Her practical discomfort, stubborn placement of the blanket, and decision to leave a loose strand of hair alone retain an adult dwarf's particular body and habits.
Friendship receives actual shared pleasure and a future place in her life, rather than a failed romance ending.
