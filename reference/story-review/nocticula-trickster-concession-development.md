Every visit is Chapter 5, in Drezen, remote through the already authored responding seal, with a 24-hour minimum delay.

1. **A signature for sale:** an invented broker sells a false guarantee invoking the Commander, Council business and access to Nocticula.
   The Commander supplies an investigation proposal rather than another request that she promise affection first.
   Nocticula receives an actual signed undertaking naming the Commander's part in the affair.
   The public option promises destruction of the reusable false pattern and restitution; the self-serving option retains a limited forwarding trade and owes copied reports before the Commander can quietly keep profitable petitions.
2. **The address he sold:** Neris obtains the broker's collection address on page.
   A one-shot Knowledge: World 35 check identifies a copied date before questioning warns the room's occupants.

   A visible investigation with the knowledgeable gate clerk is always available without a roll and also costs the secret approach.

   The private branch acquires a constrained forwarding arrangement and actually delivers its first report.
3. **The copy she keeps:** the known or missing buyers produce different accounts.
   Destroyed and retained patterns receive separate responses, and the retained business gains an additional requirement to report empty periods.
   Nocticula keeps the Commander's signature and can use its political implications later.
   Prior rejection, a surviving earlier agreement after lost patronage, and no prior agreement each receive distinct personal exchanges.
4. **An answer of her own:** Nocticula gives an affirmative personal invitation after the completed concession and several conversations.
   The Commander can decline immediately, negotiate the stated boundaries and accept, or decline after hearing the risk.
   Acceptance alone produces `noct.acq.renewed_agreement` and `noct.acq.personal_risk_accepted`.
   Her signed reply and the spent evening then produce `noct.acq.an_answer_of_her_own_done`.
