# Tirabade assembled readiness review

The current Tirabade campaign is substantial, with a convincing developed triad and meaningful separate attention to both wives.
It passes the doubled aggregate planning floor.
It is not yet a complete implementation of independently attainable romances, an optional triad and bespoke Trickster access under the project's revised requirements.
Those distinctions matter more than adding another unconnected evening to increase the count.

This is one independent assembled review, not a panel consensus or a promised score.
Only this report and the accompanying read-only measurement tool were authored for the audit.

The tool is not a Unity, native contact, ending-dispatch or save-deserialization test.

## Character and relationship assessment

Anevia is recognizably observant, irreverent and impatient with attempts to make her respectable by erasing her methods.
The early bread, the injured-leg boundary, the habit of checking doors and her refusal to surrender her private history draw on identifiable native characteristics.
Native common-dialogue cues 0016 to 0020 establish her intense attachment to Irabeth and their domestic frustrations.
The native bread dream specifically imagines Irabeth coming into the kitchen.
The new bread lesson honors that desire while letting Anevia pursue competence for herself.
Her possessiveness about her eight bowling pins, irritation after losing Vald, and interest in impressing both partners give her desires beyond facilitating somebody else's romance.

Irabeth's guarded speech, earned pride, strained relationship with usefulness and delight when she forgets to be solemn are effective.
Her travel reading, ordinary competitive game, blue coat, dance practice and initiative in arranging a room let the Commander want someone other than a wounded officer.
Native common-dialogue cues 0019 to 0027 supply the Lastwall prejudice, her distinction between Iomedae and institutional approval, the rescue of Anevia and the rebuilding of the Eagle Watch.
`i_self` develops the resulting guardedness without treating half-orc identity as a flaw to be repaired.
The later scene in which she wants the Commander to notice her in front of her wife is a particularly good transition from separate secrecy to shared desire.

The wives' attraction is already canonical; the authored task is to keep it alive while developing attraction to the Commander.
The expanded route generally does this well.
Their first dance at the lantern evening belongs to them.
Anevia arranges flattering light because she wants Irabeth to enjoy being looked at.
Irabeth wants the sketch of Anevia, chooses a coat and then chooses the later evening herself.
The wives kiss, tease and make private plans without requiring the Commander to validate every gesture.
Neither is consistently reduced to the welcoming intermediary who supplies the other.

The triad's adult intimacy is convincing and graphic and explicit.
Desire has physical expression, including hands at a waist, a deliberately prolonged kiss, the blue coat's fastenings and a bed that betrays the promised quiet.
Quiet company, sleep, returning home and postponement remain legitimate choices.
The endings of individual investigations are not simply sex rewards for agreeing with the relevant wife.
These scenes are more mature than repeatedly announcing that everyone consents, although some of the original core still relies heavily on that sort of explanation.

The cheating and negotiated triad are authored alternate developments, not recovered native romance content.
The early sources do not depict a failed marriage waiting for the Commander to repair it.
That is a strength, but it creates a larger writing obligation before both women independently choose deception.
Currently the two acquisition arcs use very similar explanations: being wanted without being useful, acknowledging love for the wife, crossing a boundary, then disliking the lie.
The aftermath is thoughtful, yet the matching temptation structure makes the dual affair feel arranged by the author more than the later marriage scenes do.
The next revision should give the women different concrete pressures and different ways of approaching the Commander, while preserving their responsibility for the choices they make.

## Concrete scope and continuity gaps

### Individual relationships are not independently attainable

`reckoning` requires `a_morning`, `i_morning`, `a_affair` and `i_affair`.
`table` requires both truth conversations and both heard flags.
`ordinary` requires that table and `trying`, and the developed campaign continues from there.
`a_roof/friend` and `i_respite/friend` both set the same global `closed` flag.
The leave/refusal branches in both crossing scenes do the same.
`Rules.Available` then closes the whole Tirabade relationship.

There are excellent individual dates inside this shared route, but no Anevia-only or Irabeth-only complete romance in the current 59 scenes.
There is also no triad entry which avoids the two secret affairs.
Calling the triad optional currently means the player can decline the entire route, not that an individual romance can remain while the group proposal is declined.
The final ordinary/provisional outcomes all concern the three together or their shared separation.

The existing betrayal route can remain a deliberate, consequential option.
For the revised scope, add independently tracked choices for each woman and a later voluntary group invitation that can be refused without closing both individual relationships.
A negotiated entry before deception would also give players a different characterization-compatible acquisition path.
Do not reinterpret old affair flags as prior spousal agreement or reuse `closed` to erase a historical breakup.

### Late installation needs a different reunion

The entire original acquisition chain can be started in Chapter 5.
`return` requires only `ordinary` plus its chapter/time conditions.
`return/now` then says the wives practiced their changed relationship while the Commander was absent, and `return/back` answers the desire to resume the relationship left behind.
On a fresh Chapter 5 romance, that shared romantic history was established after the return from the Abyss.
The source-level path is available, but the implied relationship chronology is wrong.

A reunion variant should require actual pre-departure relationship history.
A new Chapter 5 courtship should instead discuss the current situation without inventing a romantic separation.
The existing unsent-letter choice is correctly conditional; it does not solve the unconditional surrounding reunion premise.

### The core and expanded chronology need one polish pass

All 20 developed shared visits can occur in Chapter 3, including several complete shared intimate nights.
The Chapter 5 `shared_night/close` still emphasizes the unfamiliarity making all three careful.
Read after those nights, it sounds like an earlier milestone left in the old core.
That is a weaker join rather than an impossible event: familiarity can still be complicated after separation.
The prose should name that renewed uncertainty or acknowledge earlier shared nights instead of relying on generic first-time language.

`ending_loss` also uses a universal description of a future the three had scarcely learned to imagine.
Its gates permit loss after the full developed campaign and capstone.
That phrasing minimizes extensive played plans on an established history.
Use neutral grief language or developed/provisional variants, as the ordinary and ascended promise endings now do.

### Native morale and campaign consequences remain thin

Only `i_respite/start` offers the `broken` and `encouraged` branches in the inspected Tirabade scene data.
The later confident intimacy, competitive play, public assertion and future planning do not branch on those native conditions.
Joy does not have to be forbidden to an injured or demoralized woman, but the current route does not show enough of how those different versions of Irabeth arrive at that confidence.

Native Anevia cues 0044 and 0045 distinguish the Commander's violence against Irabeth and her retained scar.
Native Irabeth cue 0197 provides a specific defeated response to the Queen's death in Iz.
The new core has no comparable response to those histories.
The late private conversations should acknowledge them, permit unresolved difficulty and avoid treating a reassuring lover as a substitute for native recovery.
Likewise, the Demon and Devil power responses state limits well but do not by themselves implement consequences for later incompatible conduct.

### Trickster access is still an unimplemented requirement

The relationship's unavailable flags include either wife's death or departure, Swarm and true Lich.
The expanded shared scenes also forbid loss, inhuman state and either away flag.
The sole revival definition in the reviewed story is Seelah's, not a Tirabade recovery route.
The Trickster response in `power` is a promise not to use cosmic jokes against the women's feelings.
It is not a quest-connected intervention that retrieves an absent or dead wife.

These restrictions appropriately avoid pretending that unavailable women are present.
They leave the requested bespoke, attainable Trickster route incomplete.
A future recovery must investigate the actual Iz/departure/death history, preserve its consequences, offer a credible opportunity and establish living contact before a reunion.
Other mythics should retain the appropriate restrictions rather than inheriting a blanket resurrection bypass.

## Gameplay and pacing

The bowling ruling has real consequences: retaining the score wins the pennant and damages the immediate celebration; the replay loses the match without proving Anevia fouled.
The theft decision trades the intact travel book against the working records, and later scenes remember both the material and emotional costs.
The forged-debt investigation keeps witness knowledge distinct from suspicion and lets the Commander choose immediate partial resolution or a slower public account.
These are meaningful decisions even when no die is rolled.

There is exactly one `Check` object in the 59 scenes: Commander-only Perception DC 25 at `three_back_of_seal/method`.
Success connects the scrap to Malven; failure preserves uncertain evidence; the non-roll catalogue route costs Gresa time and produces a weaker commercial-form connection.
Later refund outcomes reflect that difference.
The romance does not end because the Commander failed to read damaged paper.
That is a useful model for future checks.

The lock lesson and bowling technique are authored choices, not hidden native Trickery or Athletics rolls.
The bowling techniques both produce six pins.
They should be described as roleplay and callbacks, not advertised as mechanical challenge diversity.
Add character-specific checks where a different approach can change an investigation or rescue; do not turn affection into a Persuasion prize.

The 20-scene optional chain has a source-level critical path of 696 hours, or 29 days, from `ordinary` to `three_rooms_unlocked` when paired individual visits occur at their earliest common time.
That calculation assumes instantaneous book-event completion and no extra travel or native interruptions.
It is a pacing lower bound, not an in-game timing test.
The long path suits a campaign spread across Chapters 3 and 5 better than repeated late-install resting just to expose the next conversation.
The short promise and explicit catch-up solve an important progression problem, but the next appointment and its remaining wait should be clear to the player.

The expanded scenes repeatedly solve a practical disagreement, hear each person separately, and reconvene for an affectionate evening.
That structure supports agency, but three consecutive cycles can become predictable.
The next substantial arc should vary the structure, for example a shared Eagle Watch problem in which Irabeth's public decision and Anevia's intelligence work each change what the other can do.
Let the player experience the formidable officer and spy as well as the considerate partners.

## Existing continuation protections and limits

`last_watch` now requires developed history or an explicitly chosen short course.
The developed ending requires `three_progression.developed`; provisional endings no longer award the finished household automatically.
`three_more_days` is an explicit Chapter 5 request to resume unfinished visits after a recorded goodbye.
The authored override preserves `last_watch` and its earlier timestamp rather than deleting the farewell.
The current progression tests inspect partial-chain catch-up, reordered individual scenes, protected native/other-romance flags and the distinction between promised and developed endings.
I reviewed those tests but did not rerun the shared suite for this literary audit.

All 59 inspected scenes lack `ContactUnit`.
Physical entries use the native Anevia/Irabeth answer lists, and shared scenes have death/departure and later away protection through the route rules.
That is meaningful protection, but it is not proof that both wives' live actors remain available during every shared scene or interruption.
The shared contact fix reviewed elsewhere does not automatically prove that behavior for scenes which do not use its contact field.
Native presentation, interruptions, saved continuation and paired physical availability remain separate verification work.

The route explicitly allows other attachments in `table/open`, `future/others` and the developed capstone.
Its narrative distinguishes deception and broken appointments from the existence of another lover.
That supports Free Love and No Jealousy coexistence without deleting the wives' own preferences or the consequences of the chosen affair story.
Source checks which preserve another relationship's flags do not certify the actual installed ToyBox settings or every parent-mod dialogue patch order.
The parent RanRomance Anevia dispatcher and its existing route invitations must remain intact.
No new artwork was reviewed here, and literary acceptance does not approve portraits, compositions or in-game crops.
