# Terendelev continuation independent review

Reviewed source: `storylines/terendelev_continuation.py`.

Exact source SHA256 reviewed: `35B1B32313537767ABDDDCD9BFE3CEDDA5ABAEC6CE7A39940907C8ECA02EC823`.

Review scope: canon versus authored continuity, identity and resurrection limits, entry histories, Trickster route, Terendelev's initiated courtship, consent, characterization, and visible choice/flag topology.

Verdict: the draft has a thoughtful, character-aware foundation, but it does not yet describe an attainable universal Trickster romance or a complete physical-return route.

This is an independent design review, not route approval, an art review, a word-count certification, or a readiness score.

## What holds up

The manuscript generally labels the RanRomance scale-split and courtship history as parent continuity, and labels the new rebuilding petition, evidence tests, and renewed courtship as authored development.

It does not turn the claw into a soul container, the undead etude name into proof of a living body, or the Ravener's death into proof of restored identity.

The native source ledger distinguishes the native consumable scale, claw collection action, native Iz `MonsterDead` etude, and parent-mod scale and Ravener flags.

The resumed returned branch uses the returned terminal cue set together with the parent romance and completed quest, and forbids the scale-bound and Lich-bound states.

The scale-confined investigation has a separate entry requiring the parent quest, bound state, Trickster path, and bound-ending history rather than assuming that an old romance etude means bodily return.

The Trickster proposal asks for evidence, tests the copied city's boundary without testing it on Terendelev, and lets her stop the experiment.

The proposed new courtship is clearly authored, is initiated by Terendelev, and allows the Commander to accept, defer, or decline.

The friendship branch states that investigative success cannot erase a previous refusal, and the other-half scenes keep the Ravener's victims and native encounter consequences visible.

The civic conflict, duty to Kenabres, concern for residents, and resistance to being used as a symbol fit the native characterization evidence cited by the research audit.

The romantic scenes include adult desire and reciprocal initiative without making a prior oath or rescue into sexual permission.

## Blocking access and branch findings

The no-parent-romance lead is not an attainable route in this source.

`trickster_native_lead` requires `terendelev.continuation.native_lead_available`, but the module neither defines that etude or flag nor sets it in a scene or integration helper.

The top-level relationship guidance calls the no-parent route an acquisition path, while the scene itself is only a research lead and supplies no present communication channel to Terendelev.

Do not expose this as a route until a specific, registered predicate and a working voluntary contact mechanism exist.

The most defensible evidence gate for an authored contact attempt is chapter and Trickster eligibility plus the actual native quest state, actual possession or verified collection history of Terendelev's original scale or claw, and the verified live-or-dead Iz Ravener result.

Those facts can justify an investigation, but they do not themselves authorize contact or prove that a soul is available.

The new route therefore needs one explicit authored mechanism that uses the verified evidence to carry a one-way invitation, then waits for Terendelev's voluntary answer before opening dialogue.

The native scale is documented as a finite consumable, so the design must prove from its native blueprint and current inventory state that it can serve this purpose before selecting it as the carrier.

If no existing native object has that documented function, the Trickster may create a new, narrowly scoped message channel as authored alternate development, but that action needs its own cost, save-safe state, tests, and registered caller rather than a text-only availability flag.

The parent friendship, rejection, and separation outcomes have a plausible scale-confined investigation entry through their cue history, and the later new-courtship page correctly requires a new affirmative answer by both characters.

That entry still relies on `RanRomTereBound` lifecycle semantics and exact cue-history behavior that the source bundle has not yet demonstrated in runtime.

The `trickster_other_half_living` page is gated by the parent-mod `RanRomTereRavener` etude alone, while its text assumes the Iz Ravener is still alive and can be resolved by the native encounter.

Pin the parent etude's activation and completion lifecycle, and add an explicit native `MonsterDead` absence check or another proved state split so a previously defeated Ravener cannot enter the living branch.

The dead branch has a distinct native death binding, which is a sounder basis, but the new binding still needs an independently reviewed loader and lifecycle test as the checkpoint itself acknowledges.

The identity review presents two evidence choices that both go to the same node and set the same final review flag.

That node narrates both a witness confirmation and claw history even when only one choice was selected, so the claimed independent evidence is not mechanically tracked.

Store and require the selected evidence separately, and only state what that branch actually verified.

The Arcana and World checks have useful failure destinations and the second-witness alternative is a reasonable fallback, but the actual check conversion and all downstream paths still need rule tests.

The boundary test page narrates a successful change and crack after a flag gate, with no modeled failed or unstable result; add an actual result branch or keep the event explicitly hypothetical until implementation.

The escape-choice page records caution, openness, or delay, but none of those outcomes returns a person or authorizes one.

The native lead, changed boundary, and other-half pages are book scenes with `Remote=True`, while the returned-person pages require the private physical contact blueprint.

The checkpoint correctly says the module is not registered in `expansion.py`, has no runtime delivery caller, and has no completed scene graph or construction tests.

## Body, identity, and arrival boundary

The draft appropriately stops short of a resurrection: no authored scene restores a body, completes a soul reunion, or changes the native Iz encounter.

The parent returned-ending cue set is not a substitute for the delivery service's confirmed actor postcondition, and the returned garden scene presumes a living Terendelev can attend.

Before making that scene available in game, require the exact already-returned outcome, the reconciled parent/native identity history, and confirmation that the intended physical actor is alive, present, and usable in the destination.

Do not narrate the letter, garden meeting, human form, or touch as a successful arrival while actor delivery is pending or unconfirmed.

The scale branch should remain contact through the existing scale only, and must not borrow physical romance prose from the returned-person branch.

The source evidence does not yet resolve scale absence or consumption, destroyed connection, released soul, incompatible terminal history, prior Lich coercion, or late installation.

Those states need separate predicates and authored outcomes before the Trickster path can be called universal.

## Actionable next revisions

Document the voluntary contact mechanism and prove that it works without a parent romance or invented replacement item.

Replace the unresolved `native_lead_available` placeholder with a real state producer, or keep the acquisition branch explicitly unavailable in all player-facing route guidance until that producer exists.

Resolve parent `RanRomTereRavener` lifecycle semantics and make living/dead branch conditions mutually exclusive against the native Iz outcome.

Make identity evidence choices record distinct prerequisites, then make the combined proof branch reachable only when its actual evidence is present.

Give the boundary experiment observable success, no-change, and unsafe outcomes, with a stop path that cannot accidentally enable crossing.

Integrate the returned route only behind the existing ending outcome and confirmed living actor, then test saved delivery and physical contact separately from the dialogue predicates.
