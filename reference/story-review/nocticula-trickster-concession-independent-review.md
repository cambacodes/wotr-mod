Either recover the wrapper on both selected unknown-buyer paths, or make the later evidence differ according to what actually survived.
The maintained tests currently check the unknown-buyers flag, not whether both producers establish the object used in the later scene.

### Establish the evidence before the date check

At `the_paid_address/start`, Neris has returned an unopened packet and an address strip.
The narration then knows that the merchant upstairs has applied the guarantee to three letters and that his initials appear beneath a date earlier than his arrival.
The room has not yet been secured, and neither a copied letter, acceptance counterfoil nor an observation of those three letters has been supplied.
The date check's object is therefore unclear: the reader cannot tell what the Commander is holding, who obtained it or why it can be compared before the raid.
The successful `read` page explains the form and scratched margin after the check, but does not supply that missing earlier access.
Give Neris or the existing gate record a concrete, limited evidence producer before the check.
Keep the three letters inside the room until a character has actually observed them, unless a witness reports them explicitly.
This is an evidence-staging weakness, not a request to add another investigation scene or more words.

## Concession value and characterization

The invented fraud is a plausible small test connected to the native Council audience, but it is not equivalent to resolving the Council, Shamira or the Worldwound.
The source mostly respects that scale.
Nocticula receives a named undertaking she can disclose, suppression or supervised reuse of a fraudulent access claim, and continuing reports if the Commander chooses the forwarding business.
She does not grant a native blessing, palace access, dream access or a settlement of the older pact in return.
Her affirmative offer concerns chosen private correspondence and future invitations.
That limited exchange is proportionate enough to support personal curiosity after the earlier earned audience and trial contact.

The signed undertaking's value is stronger on the retained-network route.
The Commander must reveal petitions before deciding which to exploit and later must account even for empty reporting periods.
Nocticula improves the bargain rather than accepting a profitable intermediary placed between her and interesting petitioners.
On the public route, her payoff is a record of the Commander's cooperation and evidence that a declared concession was performed.
The writing admits that she could have denounced the forgery through a servant.
She uses the task to examine the Commander rather than pretending this small broker threatens her rule.
Do not later treat this paper as irresistible political leverage or proof that every Council member learns the arrangement.
Its exact future cost remains an authored possibility, not a native consequence already implemented.

Nocticula corrects presumptions, distinguishes an old refusal from a new request, retains the signature after withdrawal and refuses to open the narrow fold on the invited evening.
She remains interested in useful appetites and inconvenient obligations.
The exchange about accurate reporting and the corrected advertisement gives her wit beyond repeated warnings about boundaries.
The Commander can choose exposure and restitution, a supervised profitable arrangement, refusal of the signature, or refusal of the personal invitation at two points.
The personal agreement is not granted merely because a roll succeeds.
Failed or visible investigation can still lead to it, but must retain missing buyers, an escaped assistant and continuing accounting work.

There is a literary risk in making too much of this romance consist of forms, copied lines and negotiated access.
The final evening begins to move beyond the ledger, and that is welcome.
Future visits should develop her specific ambitions, tastes and personal interest through concrete events, rather than repeat the same conversation about whether an invitation is permission.
This opening is graphic and explicit and adult, with no obligatory touch, kiss or physical meeting.
