## Local refusal versus a platonic route

A separate reachability probe shows `unborrowed_evening/leave` reaches only its own terminal.
The talk branch reaches `talk`, `want` and `honest`, never `close`.
The declined invitation completes the relevant visit and continues the investigation without that couch scene or a promised later kiss.
This reproduces the behavior described by the earlier independent review.
I did not have a byte-for-byte prior source snapshot for a textual-diff claim, so unchanged behavior is verified here rather than unqualified textual identity.

A stronger claim would be false.
In `another_place`, the declined invitation is specifically a dance: the Commander says they would rather hear what happened while they are wondering about the note.
It is not an explicit refusal of all touch or a permanent romantic withdrawal.
After listening, `others` offers an answer explicitly saying the Commander wants her and will disclose conflicting promises, leading to `promise`.
There she pauses her offered hand and the narration says the Commander closes the distance before the kiss.
That is a depicted renewed invitation and acceptance, but the acceptance is narrated rather than separately selected by the player.
The alternative answer asserts confidentiality and leads to `private`, which also narrates a kiss.
Consequently neither continuation of this conversation supplies a kiss-free completion, though the original dance is not forced and an explicit no-touch boundary is not contradicted.
The concrete control limit is that a player wishing to finish this discussion without physical contact has no such response after entry.
This is not automatically a failure merely because a continuing romance contains later contact.
If a specific dance refusal is intended to promise a wholly nonphysical visit, the current implementation does not support that stronger promise and would need a separate touch/continue-talking choice.
In `what_she_keeps`, `power` narrates a kiss, while `rival` initiates close contact for a kiss; `known` instead uses hand contact but requires prior ambition knowledge.
`no_applause/credit` offers two forms of attraction, not a platonic response.
These occur within an already accepted parent romance and do not necessarily contradict declining one particular evening.
They do mean the measured refusal-compatible completion is not evidence of an entirely nonphysical or platonic completed route.
If that stronger feature is required, add explicit compatible choices and independently recount them; do not relabel the present path.
