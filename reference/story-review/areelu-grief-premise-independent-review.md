# Areelu grief premise independent review

Date: 2026-09-26.
Scope: one independent canon and creative assessment of the research brief, selected relevant localization records, and their native dialogue blueprints.
This is not a manuscript approval, quality score, implemented route review, or live-game test.
I did not edit the brief and cannot establish the provenance of its contradictory wording.

The proposed intellectual rivalry and dangerous renewed research have strong characterization support.
The proposed inference that failed restoration makes the canonical Commander an unrelated romantic partner does not.
An evil, manipulative, or destructive adult relationship need not end in redemption to fit this game.
That creative freedom does not resolve the separate familial premise.

Earlier history also supports grief before the current experiment.
Text `bc4189f0-fda1-4cbe-9728-46ac3714dc87` describes her unsuccessful first resurrection attempt after the soul had received Pharasma's judgment and gone to the Abyss.
Text `4e1126ad-00d8-473a-97cf-8969790fce23` connects her later transformations and manipulation of demon lords to recovering the remnants and preventing another separation.
These are distinct failures at different times, not interchangeable triggers for one mandatory emotional sequence.

The script supports sadness, anger, denial, calculation, and possible change.
It does not prescribe a fixed progression through those states or require a healed final personality.
The original mortal soul is real evidence for divided identity, but its existence alone does not prove biological genealogy, untouched personal history, or the absence of a parental relationship after the graft.

## Contradictions that need correction before romantic drafting

The brief says, "Adult romantic content remains slightly-graphic and requires adults."
That conflicts with the established non-graphic scope used for this assessment.
No graphic escalation is approved by this review.

The brief requires unrelated adult participants without a parental relationship, then says, "The canonical maternal and research relationship remains a separate romantic option."
Those statements contradict each other.
The latter also conflicts with the native maternal recognition described above and the brief's own prohibition on a familial sexual or conception storyline.
The canonical maternal and research material can support a substantial non-romantic route.
It cannot be counted here as an approved romantic option.

A Commander romance needs an explicitly authored alternate continuity in which both participants are genuinely unrelated adults without a parental relationship.
A changed name, a new body, a successful soul separation, or a declaration that the restoration failed does not by itself establish that premise or remove their prior familial history.
The current brief has not supplied the alternate continuity or reconciled it with the game's experiment, revelations, and ending states.
This review does not develop a sexual or conception scenario for the familial Commander.

## Creative assessment

Intellectual rivalry is a strong starting point.
In the Aeon final dialogue, text `de516469-4e25-4f1b-8800-feed693b7d85` has Areelu study the Commander's abilities and attempt to reproduce them with a crystal and demonic allies.
That supports an opponent who learns, competes, and adjusts her experiment.
In `AreeluBurnTheWitch/Cue_0068`, asset `2d4232640a88ed9469056a0e433f71d6`, the question counter at exactly 3 accompanies a change in attention and curiosity.
Attention to a capable interlocutor is useful characterization evidence; it is not canonical romantic desire.

An authored unrelated-adult route could grow from sustained disputes over evidence, control of research, competing ambitions, and the cost of mythic power.
Areelu can lie, exploit, retaliate, or betray without automatically becoming out of character.
A destructive ending can be a successful piece of writing.
Review should examine motive, consequences, player agency, and continuity rather than demand kindness or recovery.
Defeat, dependence, compelled obedience, and the player's preferred outcome still must not be mislabeled as freely chosen attraction.

A nonsexual renewed graft fits a relapse into her established refusal to accept loss particularly well.
If she repeats an identity-replacement experiment and calls the new result her restored child, the text should acknowledge that continuity of motive instead of presenting it as proven acceptance.
The Commander can support, oppose, sabotage, or bargain over that project as an authored dangerous research branch.
An evil alliance need not abandon it merely to qualify for a favorable editorial review.

Acceptance is a different possible outcome, not a compulsory destination.
A preservation experiment that accepts uncertainty, protects the existing person's identity, and abandons the demand to reproduce her child could express change.
Those safeguards and that metaphysical possibility are authored developments unless separately established by native evidence.
A successful graft is not itself evidence that she has changed, and a failed graft need not force her to change.
An unresolved alliance, a deliberate return to destructive research, and refusal to continue are all viable non-romantic conclusions for the canonical relationship.

Trickster can expose an assumption about fate or create an opportunity to contest it.
The reviewed evidence does not establish a native Trickster power that automatically creates a new unrelated history, resurrects an intact lost soul, or makes Areelu attracted to the Commander.
Such an intervention needs a specific authored mechanism, earned access, and consequences.
Other mythic branches should retain their actual restrictions rather than borrow Gold Dragon reconciliation or Legend hostility as if they were universal scenes.

## Questions the next design revision must answer

1. Where exactly does the unrelated-adult alternate continuity diverge, and which native revelations and familial scenes become unavailable or receive explicit alternate treatment?
2. Which soul or remnants are present at each step, and what distinguishes separation, preservation, restoration, and replacement in the route's own rules?
3. Which prior choices and mythic states allow an encounter with a living, available Areelu, and which existing endings remain binding?
4. What quest evidence and achieved Trickster intervention unlock the alternate path without making attraction a magical reward?
5. What does a renewed experiment risk, who can refuse it, and which endings intentionally represent relapse, ambition, acceptance, or betrayal?
6. Which checks alter information, leverage, or consequences, and how does later dialogue remember the result without reducing the route to passing one persuasion roll?

The premise is ready for a corrected design revision and deeper branch mapping.
It is not ready for romantic manuscript approval or an assertion that a canon-compatible unrelated Commander branch already exists.
