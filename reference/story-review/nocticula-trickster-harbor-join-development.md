Independent canon/creativity review must judge whether the extrapolation is credible enough.

The demonstrated exit is selected dialogue and a narrated awakening; it is not proof of an engine-level escape from arbitrary native dream control.
All new flags use `noct.join.` and none writes a native alias.
The scenes forbid death, Council conflict, acquisition closure and already-closed harbor history.
