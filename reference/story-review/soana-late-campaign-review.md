## Findings

The return remembers either saved saplings or the failed nursery, rather than silently improving the earlier choice.
The clapper in the friendship farewell is supported by the earlier cord-and-clapper warning experiment.
The sealed bowl, interrupted voice and retained practical mistrust are acknowledged without pretending the later thorn undoes the earlier rite.
Meret brings the report she chose to give and leaves with her own people; her grandmother's obligation does not become permanent service.

The snares lead to a concrete tracking check with a failure cost and a slower non-roll approach.
The exclusion and limited-harvest choices each impose a cost that remains in the later account.
Soana retains her self-interest and capacity to frighten trespassers; she does not become agreeable merely because the Commander has a reasonable objection.
The new protective working is explicitly an authored limited rite, not a replacement for Orso's binding or a world-state restoration.
The later romantic conversation preserves Corven's unknown status and distinguishes partnership, continuing courtship and friendship after romance.
The farewell gives the player distinct affectionate choices without using romance to settle the dispute over Orso.
