In platform_finished/loan, saying Orsa wants an agreement between equals still leads to unfinished beginning with Seelah's wish to have made it simple.
That can be inferred from her nearly returning the payment, but it does not answer the player's observation directly.

Likewise, the other loan choice reaches same and hears "I would still help," although the player did not ask whether she would help again.
These are weak conversational joins, not contradictory events or dead ends.

Retain one of these explicit admissions and let the practical response carry more of the others.

The Elan memory says she wants to tell a story without making it a lesson, then postpones that story.

No points are awarded here for the unwritten story or the proposed conversation with living Elan.
