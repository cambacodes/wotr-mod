## What now works

`the_price_of_her_name` and `the_paper_she_kept` give Minagho a problem appropriate to the general who helped ruin Drezen.
She recognizes genuine orders, admires a blackmailer's business model, objects to his incompetence, and wants useful sources rather than a comforting acquittal.
The cult, redemption, dragon, legend and service responses have different reasons for caring about the same papers.
The Commander can insist that a false accusation be corrected without pretending the authentic order was innocent.
The knowledge check changes how the false document is exposed, and the purchase alternative leaves a cost for the follow-up to remember.
Her gloves and Chivarro's book make the aftermath personal without a sudden conversion speech.

Chivarro's revised preview bargain is an especially useful correction.
She doubles the buyer's price, invents competing offers, and keeps Sivane on the agreed fee.
Sivane's displeasure survives into the later negotiation over future work.
The refusal branch lets Chivarro enjoy humiliating a customer while admitting she wanted his money.
This is closer to the hostess who used hospitality, deception and intimidation in the native game than a uniformly benevolent arts patron would be.
It need not reproduce every native cruelty in every private meeting to remain recognizable.

The romance also improves through particulars.
Minagho's collar and table-game exchanges feel different from Chivarro's unreliable couch, improvised blanket and theatrical teasing.
The women kiss, compete, share food, remember each other's habits and irritate each other without waiting for the Commander to explain their attraction.
The separate-room dispute and returned book give their relationship a small history within this continuation.
The service restriction and separate commitments protect their choices mechanically; the best scenes let those mechanics support the dialogue quietly.

The opening inquiry, invitation intervention, merchant encounter, document exposure, preview bargain and later performer negotiation provide more than a sequence of affectionate readings.
Failures or alternative approaches can change costs, public embarrassment and later dialogue without making a single failed roll erase the entire romance.
The new Trickster intervention is tied to the witnessed parent brand trick and a particular copied invitation.
It does not simply award both women as a mythic reward.

## Required editorial revision

The manuscript repeatedly explains a relationship principle after a concrete interaction has already demonstrated it.
This persists across `the_answer_after_business`, the room scene, the later commitment discussion and `before_the_last_road`.
Invitations, direct answers, separate choices and promises become a shared abstract vocabulary for both women and the narrator.
The cumulative effect is stronger than any one awkward sentence.

For example, `what_she_will_take/visits` has Chivarro explain that she would rather hear an answer than improve the Commander's promise in her imagination and resent the improvement.
The following `visits_close` then explains that the invitation can be kept without promising every shore together.
The farewell returns to separate answers, future invitations and avoiding an unnecessarily binding departure.
These passages protect essentially the same choice several times instead of showing what Chivarro now wants to do with it.

Retain the actual choices and safeguards.
Cut the explanatory restatements after the choice has become clear, especially narrator assurances that a character is not imposing some additional obligation.
Where a scene loses its dramatic purpose after that cut, replace the explanation with a particular disagreement, desire, practical inconvenience or unfinished scheme belonging to that woman.
The new forged-orders sequence and Sivane fee dispute demonstrate the needed standard already.
The Commander should occasionally have to interpret an answer rather than receive a polished explanation of its full emotional meaning.
This does not require adding arbitrary cruelty or removing affectionate development.

The final farewell should consolidate the decisions earned earlier instead of conducting another long general discussion of how invitations and independent relationships work.
Chivarro's plans for her work and Minagho's concrete ambitions can carry more of that weight.
After revision, recount both individual budgets; shortening repetitive material must not silently take either below 21,000 meaningful corpus words.

## Required continuity corrections

1. In `a_room_she_likes/separate`, line 978 hands the Commander the overfilled cup.
   Shared node `tea`, line 980, hands over a cup again and says Chivarro lost track of the tea while considering the pins.
   Only the alternative `notice` branch considers pins; `separate` has her distracted by the argument with Minagho.
   Move the handoff to the common page or make each incoming branch complete its own staging, and use a common reason for the strong tea or a genuinely conditional recollection.
   Verify both `choose -> notice -> tea` and `choose -> separate -> tea` as actual text sequences.

2. In `the_first_small_audience/counter`, line 1363 tells Sivane her fee was agreed yesterday.
   The agreement occurs in `the_performer_and_the_key/fee`.
   Six mandatory 24-hour transitions separate that visit from the preview: rehearsal, room, forged orders, paper aftermath, musician and preview.
   Remove the unsupported relative date or explicitly establish a later renewed agreement.
   A long player delay must also remain coherent.

The revised pursuit sentence at line 208 deserves a small additional correction.
It now says the last pursuit ended before it reached Minagho.
The parent dragon and legend variants describe assassins striking during training, with a dragon or troops intervening.
The replacement fixes the original assertion that every pursuer died, but its new spatial claim remains unnecessarily questionable on those histories.
Use the already accurate idea that the last pursuit failed, without asserting that the attackers never reached her.

One lesser staging ambiguity remains in the forged-document success page.
Minagho says she has paid for a close inspection at line 1132, although the visible payment before that branch buys two admissions and the separate inspection price is paid only on the purchase path.
If she is deliberately claiming admission entitles her to inspect the paper, give that bluff a readable cue; otherwise make the payment wording match the selected path.
This is a clarification request rather than evidence of another unpaid gameplay transaction.
