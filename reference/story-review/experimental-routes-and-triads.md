# Experimental routes and optional triads

This is a developmental concept brief, not delivered romance content and not a scored review.
The revised scope prioritizes attainable Trickster routes while allowing character-appropriate restrictions on other mythic paths.
Existing RanRomance and base-game romances should supply their own starts and progress; additions should attach new intersections instead of creating competing first romances.
Ember and Aivu receive nonromantic companion stories only.

## Soana: a visitor who can leave

Native facts: Soana is an elderly shaman and grandmother, not a young forest enchantress.
Roan's letter, localization `7a990439-b4f8-41db-91ad-2e80b9c08c83`, describes children and grandchildren, her refusal to leave, and an intended spirit-binding ritual.
Her own speech at `b3e65c8e-8cdb-4350-9189-1a333fd51709` identifies pride and shame at returning to her children after failing the forest.
At `1954736b-4e33-40da-bb2d-4892c6695ef7`, she calls the Commander a bloody hunter and threatens them in defense of the forest.
The native forced service described at `68ee13a9-a6d6-4301-a77a-5ed173c0896b` and the 'support and love' summary at `b549a20f-2a12-424e-87f4-fb57a9b4a8bc` establish coercion, not a romantic opening.
These references are preserved in `Soana-concept-evidence.txt`.

Invented arc: begin with removal of compelled service and a dispute over an actual damaged place in the forest.
She should be allowed to refuse the Commander's solution and keep calling out their hunting or careless power.
The second meeting concerns a small ritual she can still perform herself, with the Commander assisting rather than replacing her expertise.
The first affectionate beat is her invitation to return without work to do, followed by an acerbic admission that she noticed the absence.
A later meeting concerns whether to answer her family, never an obligation to reconcile as the price of romance.
Give her tactile, direct desire and occasional inconvenient temper; preserve age, weathering, weak eyesight, and history in art.
Do not make restored youth the reward for becoming desirable.

Invented Trickster intervention: invalidate the command that makes her serve, then reopen a road home or a lost message route.
If she was killed, physical restoration needs a real recovery action and a separate reckoning with who killed her.
Restoration supplies an opportunity to act freely; it does not supply an affectionate flag.
A quiet refusal can keep the forest correspondence alive without continuing romance.

## Areelu: the person outside the hypothesis

Native facts: Areelu seeks to restore her child through the Commander's altered soul.
Localization `3e33bf90-7bac-46ec-9b26-70aa22336522` describes tethering her child's remnants to a mortal soul.
At `ccebf842-d6a0-4f67-8dab-1d00a5751fd8`, she explicitly cannot think of the Commander as anything but her child.
At `bd28132f-e15b-4825-929a-7b442115bfc1`, she is prepared to kill the current Commander to begin her experiment again.
Targona describes imprisonment and experiments at `335022e1-ee41-47eb-a8e7-7f3eecfa1fca`.
Her survival or apparent attachment cannot erase these relationships and crimes.
These references are preserved in `Areelu-concept-evidence.txt`.

The strongest canon-adjacent experimental route is nonromantic: adversarial research, attempted recognition of the Commander as a separate person, and unresolved attachment after the experiment fails.
Its central scene should demand that she hear an answer she did not design, then show whether she changes her conduct toward a surviving victim.
Her voice should remain exact, impatient with empty moral theater, and capable of startling personal grief without becoming a repentant mother in every exchange.
She can relinquish a piece of control without receiving absolution.

A romance concept would require an explicitly alternate premise involving genuinely unrelated adults, with no established parental relationship between them.
Simply declaring that the Commander is not her biological child, separating the soul fragment, or changing the word she uses is insufficient development for that premise.
Do not romanticize the native maternal bond or sell its erasure as a spicy fate trick.
If an unrelated-adult AU is later authorized and actually authored, the promising attraction is intellectual rivalry that survives her relinquishing control of the other person's identity.
Keep that AU separate from the canon-adjacent reconciliation route and clearly label the invention.
A Trickster recovery can preserve her life for a confrontation; it cannot by itself establish either this alternate identity or mutual attraction.

## Introductions and triad prospects

All attraction proposed below is new writing unless explicitly identified as an established relationship.
An introduction is not evidence of native romance or mutual attraction.
Each prospective pair needs a scene together without the Commander directing their flirtation, a private chance for each to decline, and a later three-person invitation.
A woman may want the Commander and dislike the proposed partner, or love both but prefer separate relationships.
Those outcomes should remain valid under Love Is Free and Jealousy Begone.

| Pair or intersection | Invented attraction and scene | Required conflict or limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kiana and Aranka | Collaborate on a disastrous rehearsal; Aranka admires Kiana's timing, and Kiana notices the singer staying after everyone leaves. | Kiana's separation or grief remains present; Aranka is not a replacement for Elan or a cure for bereavement. |
| Seelah and Arueshalae | Shared work for ordinary people becomes a conversation neither initially calls a date. | Integrate Arueshalae's native arc and let Seelah admire effort without treating her as a redemption project. |
| Gesmerha and Soana | Two elders disagree over how a damaged community should live, then discover pleasure in each other's uncompromising company. | Gesmerha must not excuse spirit abuse, and Soana must not claim authority over the chief; no blindness or age cure. |
| Konomi and Galfrey | Political letters become an invitation where each has to stop addressing the other's office. | Rank and loyalty are real pressures; either can reject a triad while keeping her individual route. |
| Jerribeth and Vellexia | An existing connection permits an introduction, then a newly authored attraction grows through rivalry over a shared artwork. | Native servant or companion history is not proof of an existing romance; Vellexia cannot purchase Jerribeth's participation. |
| Wenduag and Nurah | A practical alliance exposes mutual suspicion of benevolent masters; attraction appears in what they choose to reveal. | Do not turn shared exploitation into instant tenderness or imply that fear of punishment is willingness. |
| Arsinoe and Kiana | Work on invitations for people recovering from the soul crisis lets practical restraint meet theatrical generosity. | Allow real religious and social disagreement over the separation; Arsinoe is not the marriage counselor who claims a patient. |
| Targona and Terendelev | An invented post-recovery meeting explores what each can remember of bodily violation and continued service. | Requires actual recovery and independent availability; shared suffering alone is not chemistry. |
| Nocticula and an independent adult Areelu AU | A possible later intellectual and political rivalry, only if the unrelated-adult premise exists first. | Not a shortcut around Areelu's parental context or Targona's victimization, and not assumed native lovers. |

Minagho and Chivarro already have a relationship and an existing RanRomance triad route, so extend their mutual decisions and ordinary shared life rather than writing a duplicate recruitment sequence.
Anevia and Irabeth likewise need continued three-person and two-person scenes that acknowledge their established marriage.
Camellia should receive intersections through her existing route, but she is a poor default partner for Soana: native content explicitly permits giving Soana to her, and Soana reacts to her blood scent with disgust at `ac6d8542-f7ed-4647-8c06-0592434da6ed`.
A Camellia intersection should expose that conflict rather than use a shared spiritual vocabulary to fabricate affinity.
Wenduag can make introductions for advantage, but each introduced woman should discover and answer that motive in her own voice.

Start with Kiana-Aranka, Jerribeth-Vellexia, and Gesmerha-Soana as three substantially different prototypes: playful collaboration, dangerous aesthetic rivalry, and mature disagreement over a community.
Do not attach every woman to every other woman merely to maximize combinations.
An honest pair rejection is useful authored content and should not end unrelated romances.
Future reviewers should score the actual pair scenes, individual refusals, and triad continuations separately from the underlying solo routes.
