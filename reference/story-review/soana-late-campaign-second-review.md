## What supports acceptance

`what_the_hollow_costs/reserve` lets Soana take most of the returned food despite knowing the travelers' need.
Mava's accusation remains unanswered by a convenient rescue, and `the_days_she_counted/reserve` lets Soana defend the decision again.
The harvest alternative costs her three mornings and disturbance of the habitat; it does not make the travelers content with every limit.
This keeps a forest-first witch in the scene rather than turning her into a uniformly generous companion.
Her willingness to frighten strangers also survives the warning test.
The softer choice increases the work she must do, while the stronger choice retains its force and must surrender some reach after the boundary check.

`a_track_with_two_ends/start` uses an actual Commander Lore Nature DC 26 check, with success and failure destinations and a non-roll approach.

The slower choice avoids that additional mistake but cannot finish the inspection before dark.
The later account allows the dog to recover enough to walk only part of the road without pretending that a native healing mechanic was executed.
These are book-event consequences, and the source does not claim otherwise.

`where_the_steps_end/changed_role` is a necessary, credible distinction from the earlier interrupted voice working.
Soana remembers saying that someone else would close a future bowl.
She now gives the Commander a different task with a stop condition either can enforce, and explains why this task does not place her voice under the same threat.
That preserves practical mistrust without requiring a permanent ban on working together.
The ordinary role branch separately admits that her earlier use of a smaller sign frightened someone she wanted to welcome.
That admission serves this experiment's staging and does not manufacture a native spell history.

The thorn is explicitly temporary, narrow and vulnerable to rain or a wet cloth.
The source identifies no enslaved creature in it and no promise of a secure forest.
`the_days_she_counted/guardian`, `bound` and `dead` explicitly preserve the native life knot or Orso's absence.
The Commander can continue objecting while pursuing the romance.
The story therefore develops a smaller protective practice without using it as a covert Orso cure or complete moral rehabilitation.

Meret's grandmother's mark remains remembered help rather than hereditary service.
Her departure is established in both harvest and reserve branches before later scenes refer to it.
Hessa and Mava stay to inspect the boundary separately, so their presence at the test does not require Meret to have remained as well.
The zero-delay test supports that immediate sequence, although arbitrary real-world delays between book events remain an engine scheduling limitation rather than an implemented timed appointment.

`the_days_she_counted/letter` neither delivers the bark nor finds Corven's grave or permission.
The partnership conversation explicitly acknowledges that Soana has not become unmarried by writing it.
She can choose a present relationship while accepting that she cannot speak for him.
This is an authored alternate development with a difficult unresolved fact, not a false assertion of a canonical open marriage.
Other loves are allowed in the proposed shared life without changing other romance flags.

The last evening contains bodily interest and play rather than only a negotiation about relationship rules.
