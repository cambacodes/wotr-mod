## Voice, friendship and independent purpose

The opening recognizes Aivu through behavior instead of relying on repeated cookie references.
She draws too many birds, questions the usefulness of a former bucket shop as a landmark, and wants the city to be wonderful as well as navigable.
Her demand that the map remain hers gives the whole sequence a purpose she chose herself.
The Commander can contribute practical help without converting her drawing into an official military map.

The accident is particularly effective because she admits it before being questioned.
Her first response still overpromises a complete repair, and her annoyance when the shirts remain dirty after an apology sounds like a young person discovering what repair entails.
She can understand that Jori is frightened while remaining frustrated that his feelings do not recover as quickly as the awning.
The pole comparison expresses that frustration concretely.
Nessa gives her work and limits without humiliating her; Jori is permitted to remain wary.

The narrow passage supplies a present-tense fear without importing the later native kidnapping confession at `8a01a519-d581-488f-9842-b560129e848a`.
Nobody claims she has been rescued, gives her later memories or changes her age to make the scene possible.
She can help from outside, take a break, or leave the obstruction for somebody better equipped.
Her value as a friend never depends on demonstrating that a dragon cannot be frightened.

The last scene shows an independent social life beginning to form.
Jori responds to the specific earlier repair approach; Aivu asks him for future help with roofs and lettering.
She may keep exploring before making Nessa a copy, and Nessa accepts that her map is not an obligation owed immediately to everyone who finds it useful.
The arrow-stone is a specific shared reminder, while her plans continue when the Commander leaves.
This meets the friendship brief's demand that she be more than a morale reward or a prop in someone else's adult relationships.

There is no romantic courtship, sexual framing, adult redesign, lover flag or dependency on the Commander's number of partners.
The common relationship schema names a CommittedFlag, but its actual value is `aivu.trusted`, earned as friendship.
No romance intensity score applies to this character.

## Plot, props and player decisions

The four scenes form a connected small story: invent the map, face harm caused while extending it, investigate a useful route, then let another person test the result.
Each scene changes the next scene's circumstances.
The second visit's accident does not merely create an apology button; its physical and social repair receives a later response from Jori.
The third visit's cart remains blocked if the player declines the job, and the fourth visit explicitly tests the marked detour instead of claiming an unearned clearance.

The map-format choice is also more than cosmetic.
Combining names creates an overlarge decorative sock that obscures a turn; putting names on opposite sides makes orientation confusing when the sheet is flipped.
Both are plausible problems in the chosen design rather than a preferred answer being secretly perfect.
The laundry user's feedback makes the player's earlier decision matter without invalidating Aivu's imagination.

Perception DC18 offers a real check with different narrated work afterward.
Success identifies a safe adjustment and preserves the cart; failure reaches the slower dismantling with Nessa.
The non-roll choice can deliberately take that slower course, and leaving the cart in place has a separate completed outcome.
Friendship progresses in each case because the objective is a trustworthy map and shared work, not proving the Commander's statistics.
The final copy-versus-exploration decision records two different next intentions; this contribution does not pretend either future activity has already been played.

Some early answer pairs converge immediately, such as choosing to hear Jori or ask Nessa for tools.
They allow a tone choice but should not be counted as distinct plot outcomes.
The longer-lived map, repair and route decisions provide the actual replay value.
The supporting people, repaired awning, cart and stone are book-scene narrative objects, not implemented NPCs, scenery changes or inventory rewards.

The prose has enough concrete work and humor to avoid feeling like a list of lessons.
Its main risk over a longer arc is repeating the pattern of an excited mistake followed by a patient adult's correction.
A later outing should let Aivu's unusual observation solve something the Commander misunderstood, or let her initiate a game that stays a game.
The Commander currently contributes mostly supervision and labor; a later reciprocal scene should offer a specific preference, uncertainty or need that Aivu can respond to in her own imperfect way.
These are directions for expansion, not reasons to insert padding into this opening.

## Depth and assessment

This is a substantial opening with an earned local payoff.
