## Blocking branch joins

1. noct.white_shoes/seventh lets every Trickster say there is a seventh line in the accounts.
That fact is disclosed only in noct.captains_reply/ledger, reached from the successful ledger investigation.
A Trickster who chose the direct bait or contradictory-buyers method can enter seventh without having heard it.
Supply the observation in a common report before the choice, or require the actual learned fact.

2. noct.cost_of_return/start introduces a kitchen as belonging to Teren's chosen witness and describes his sister's appointment.
Teren is introduced only in noct.voices_in_glass/volunteer.
The Ilvara-carrier path bypasses that introduction, yet receives the same familiar reference to him and his arrangements.
The phrase "whether or not he makes the crossing" does not establish who he is or why this household enters the plan.
Give the common scene an actual introduction or use branch-specific opening material.

3. noct.hearing/business describes Nocticula's expression as the same one she showed when the Commander proposed contradictory buyers.
Its only guard is Trickster.
The ledger and bait methods remain valid predecessors, and neither proposed those buyers.
Use a common prior incident or gate the exact memory.

4. noct.her_own_face/night says "The queen in that history" and jokes about the drainage chapters.
The queen's affair and drainage material is told in talk, which goes to morning and never reaches night.
The face branch has only seen an unidentified book, while invention has heard a different dynasty and ford anecdote.
The intimate branch therefore recalls the content of the branch it did not take.

## Voice, prose, and attraction

Nocticula has several convincing moments.
Her impatience at the Commander finding an accounting answer first in lamp_measure/read makes the brief kiss feel like competitive pleasure.
The threat against Dessa's employer in captains_reply/threat gives her a concrete use for her reputation.
Her response to the limited crossing keeps her appetite for the harbor distinct from her attraction to the person who cost her it.
The parent likewise allows practical bargains, uncertain attachment, and explicit nonexclusivity, so a developed private interest is a sound alternate continuation.

The sustained problem is how often the narrator then explains that the good outcome did not make her good, the kiss did not settle the argument, the prisoner was useful rather than converted, or the ending did not guarantee everything else.
Repeated explanation starts supplying the character's danger on her behalf instead of letting the scene demonstrate it.
Nocticula becomes the patient administrator of almost every safeguard and payment while the prose insists that she remains dangerous.
Her present choices can be pragmatic, but their motives need to be particular enough that they are recognizable as hers without a recurring moral qualification.

Concrete places requiring an editorial pass include:

- noct.demonstration/witness calls the bell "not an enchanted rescue waiting conveniently in a story."
This comments on the author's construction of the plot from inside the narrated world.
- noct.what_she_keeps/confined promises no miraculous discovery that would make the decision secretly the most profitable choice.
That is a review assurance, not an observed event.
- noct.what_she_keeps/exiled says no ending has arrived to prove the choice right or wrong.
The uncertainty already follows from the limited report; naming the absence of an ending weakens it.
- noct.what_she_keeps/known explains that affection guarantees no unearned ending.
The actual Worldwound dispute belongs in the dialogue, while this sentence belongs in the implementation notes.
- noct.second_door/power repeats that desire is not payment and work is not a test, then explains how the two may remain entangled.
At the culmination, another analysis of the arrangement substitutes for a specific intimate exchange.

The solution is a sustained edit, not deletion of only these five sentences.
Preserve the characters' actions, prices, and remembered disagreements; remove commentary that repeats their lesson.
Use the recovered space for actual private interaction, an uncomfortable demand, a revealing lapse, or a distinctly Nocticula kind of pleasure.
The route does not need graphic sexuality to become more charged.
Its strongest romantic material already shows how competitive intelligence, deliberate touch, and a withheld answer can work.

The existing Profane Gift deserves one concrete treatment in this development.
Every visit mechanically requires it, and native material explicitly establishes the possibility of mental influence.
Yet the playable text never names that continuing leverage while repeatedly describing invitations, privacy, and freedom from obedience.
This does not mean the Commander is automatically compelled in each scene, nor that every meeting needs a consent speech.
A specific exchange should distinguish her choice not to invoke a real power here from the power having disappeared or become irrelevant because the relationship is warmer.
Keep the actual patronage mechanics unchanged.

## Decisions and authored magic

The harbor rules form an intelligible invented mystery: stolen permission, unfinished arrivals, return witnesses, and a door whose reinforcement has a price.
The distinction between a lamp and a soul avoids using the incident as an unsupported resurrection claim.
Nocticula's avoidance of entering a possible permanent anchor herself is a credible practical limit.
The limited Trickster solution requires preparation and gives up the acquired passage rather than merely winning every outcome for free.
These are strengths of this authored incident, not evidence that the native game already supports such a spell.

The strongest remembered consequences are the warned collector, the choice of carrier, Vessa's injury under the weaker crossing, the three chart outcomes, and the three dispositions of Ilvara.
The injured-survivor scene does not make compensation erase harm.
That is substantive aftermath rather than numerical filler.
The initial rescue, power, and curiosity motives also return in the final visit.

There are three Commander-only Knowledge: World checks at DC 30, 32, and 33.
The first changes the investigative method, and the last may lose the chance to respond quietly before an accusation spreads.
The middle check in noct.hearing has a weaker consequence contract.
Success writes noct.hearing_proof and failure writes noct.hearing_uncertain; the two direct alternatives write noct.hearing_limits or noct.hearing_business.
None of these four flags is read later in the manuscript.
Failure promises that employment will require someone to watch the unexplained defense, but the commission and final reports do not distinguish that risk.
Remember it through an actual later report or cost, or stop suggesting that the failure created a distinct oversight requirement the route then forgets.
Adding another unused effect flag would not fix it.

Most of the investigation occurs through Nocticula's reports after her agents perform the physical work.
That is consistent with the restricted dream-delivery scope, and the source correctly avoids making the Commander retroactively change a completed crossing.
It also creates a pacing limit.
The private scenes need more variety and immediate interaction because the political action itself is usually reported afterward.
The Commander should not receive narrative credit for work Rhez, Vessa, or a carrier performed, and the current public-account passages correctly avoid that error.
