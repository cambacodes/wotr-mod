## Voice, prose, and attraction

Nocticula has several convincing moments.
Her impatience at the Commander finding an accounting answer first in lamp_measure/read makes the brief kiss feel like competitive pleasure.
The threat against Dessa's employer in captains_reply/threat gives her a concrete use for her reputation.
Her response to the limited crossing keeps her appetite for the harbor distinct from her attraction to the person who cost her it.
The parent likewise allows practical bargains, uncertain attachment, and explicit nonexclusivity, so a developed private interest is a sound alternate continuation.

The sustained problem is how often the narrator then explains that the good outcome did not make her good, the kiss did not settle the argument, the prisoner was useful rather than converted, or the ending did not guarantee everything else.
Repeated explanation starts supplying the character's danger on her behalf instead of letting the scene demonstrate it.
Nocticula becomes the patient administrator of almost every safeguard and payment while the prose insists that she remains dangerous.
Her present choices can be pragmatic, but their motives need to be particular enough that they are recognizable as hers without a recurring moral qualification.

Concrete places requiring an editorial pass include:

- noct.demonstration/witness calls the bell "not an enchanted rescue waiting conveniently in a story."
This comments on the author's construction of the plot from inside the narrated world.
- noct.what_she_keeps/confined promises no miraculous discovery that would make the decision secretly the most profitable choice.
That is a review assurance, not an observed event.
- noct.what_she_keeps/exiled says no ending has arrived to prove the choice right or wrong.
The uncertainty already follows from the limited report; naming the absence of an ending weakens it.
- noct.what_she_keeps/known explains that affection guarantees no unearned ending.

- noct.second_door/power repeats that desire is not payment and work is not a test, then explains how the two may remain entangled.

The solution is a sustained edit, not deletion of only these five sentences.
Preserve the characters' actions, prices, and remembered disagreements; remove commentary that repeats their lesson.
Use the recovered space for actual private interaction, an uncomfortable demand, a revealing lapse, or a distinctly Nocticula kind of pleasure.
The route does not need graphic sexuality to become more charged.
Its strongest romantic material already shows how competitive intelligence, deliberate touch, and a withheld answer can work.

The existing Profane Gift deserves one concrete treatment in this development.
Every visit mechanically requires it, and native material explicitly establishes the possibility of mental influence.
Yet the playable text never names that continuing leverage while repeatedly describing invitations, privacy, and freedom from obedience.
This does not mean the Commander is automatically compelled in each scene, nor that every meeting needs a consent speech.
A specific exchange should distinguish her choice not to invoke a real power here from the power having disappeared or become irrelevant because the relationship is warmer.
Keep the actual patronage mechanics unchanged.
