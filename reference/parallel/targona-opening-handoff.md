## Played content and consequences

Targona begins by asking the Commander to examine a drawing she keeps making of a laboratory room.
The drawing is her new authored recollection, not a recovered native document or a guaranteed accurate room plan.
The player chooses what to notice and how to reply personally.
The next letter introduces a disagreement over a collector's request for an uplifting account of captivity.
Publishing her own imperfect account and withholding it both have later benefits and costs, with different replies and reported reader reactions.
Neither choice turns her involuntary imprisonment into a noble obligation she should have welcomed.

A paper-door construction offers a native Commander-only Perception25 check, with distinct success, failure, and no-roll cutting approaches.
Success yields a usable fold; failure returns a damaged attempt honestly; the alternate cuts a new door without pretending that this was possible in the actual prison.
Targona's next reply reacts to the exact result and supplies her own work, rather than having the Commander solve her distress for her.
The DC reflects a specialized fine-line inspection, using the local native Perception25 romance-inspection scale as a balance reference, not a recovered Targona DC.
Failure does not deny courtship or the next scene.
There is no inventory reward, XP, physical lockpick action, or skill roll that buys affection.

The later letters write an ordinary fictional woman whose decisions belong to her, with leaving and receiving branches.
Current Trickster can give a copied page an impossible additional door, a new authored use of mythic power limited to the copy.
The original remains intact, the trick is optional, and the result does not restore a prisoner, heal a wing, rewrite native history, or cure recurring distress.
The existing implemented RanRomance Trickster transformation supplies the route's attainable quest connection; this small incident extends it without falsely granting Angel quest outcomes.
An ordinary continuation remains fully playable for Trickster and every other supported history.
Targona's final reply acknowledges the limited result, expresses her own preferences, and asks for continued affection or friendship according to the actual parent state.
No new commitment, marriage, physical encounter, or completed final campaign arc is awarded.
