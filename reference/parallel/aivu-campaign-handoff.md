# Aivu friendship campaign handoff

This contribution extends the existing four map visits into a sustained friendship campaign, with two optional Nexus visits and separate epilogues.
Aivu remains the game's young havoc dragon throughout.
There is no romance, sexualization, age transformation, combat recruitment, native rescue completion, or newly granted mythic power.
The author supplies evidence and tests, not an independent approval or score.

## Trickster guest contract and remaining scope

The installed capital mechanics belong to current Azata, and the Nexus override requires current Azata too.
Neither supplies a Trickster guest actor.
The native `AivuWithLegend` etude `1ce861b6b2e110048b89d7d19fc52826` translocates and unhides a particular capital proxy; it does not establish the same opportunity for Trickster.
The native Shyka anecdote is a credible future connection for a misplaced invitation and limited safe meeting, as proposed in the full companion brief.
Implementing that route requires a verified guest delivery mechanism, actual contact, identity reuse when the native Aivu already exists, and a voluntary new introduction for a Commander she has never met.
It must distinguish loss of power, banishment, estrangement and lethal histories before authoring any return.
This module supplies none of those mechanics and does not mark their requirement complete.

The complete ordinary campaign can be independently judged now, while universal Trickster acquisition, unavailable-history recovery, rendered delivery, save/reload UI checks, and scene art remain separate outstanding work.
A fresh Chapter 4 installation without the authored opening does not acquire the new campaign solely from the rescue scene; fresh Chapter 5 access is provided explicitly.
No new spoken voice performance, live die execution, inventory prop, or rendered portrait crop was tested here.
Suggested age-appropriate art subjects are Aivu with the garden map, the tail-tag game, the weathered sign, the leaf instrument, and quiet Nexus company.
Her native dragon design and stage-appropriate size must remain intact.
