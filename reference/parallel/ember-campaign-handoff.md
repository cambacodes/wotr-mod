## Characterization and authored alternatives

The existing friendship brief and both existing modules were read before authoring.
The campaign continues Ember's interest in ordinary play without making every activity another performance.
Her compassion can coexist with disappointment, impatience, mistaken expectations, and concern about what she cannot answer.
The Commander receives specific care in return, choosing a personal admission, comic distraction, or silence.

Native anchors include her street life, traumatic history, missing fingers that she does not ask to replace, Soot's companionship, direct criticism of wealth without practical help, refusal to become the Redeemed's commander, and her distress about frightening people on the law outcome.
The devastated native state is not followed by confident sermons or a miraculous restoration of her powers.
Care visits do not award `trusted_friend` or ordinary campaign completion, and they preserve a pre-existing friendship flag if it was already earned.
The ongoing-care marker records permission to ask for future visits, not proof of recovery or forgiveness.

Soot is female.

Vessa, Deren, Mara, Heda, Tessa, Oren, Selvi, Leth, and Anet are authored civilians.
The room, lost objects, covering dispute, letters, and new jokes are authored events.
Deren is a former thief who says what he did; he is not asserted to be a particular native cultist spared by a quest choice.
The missing covering has an actual borrower, an actual cost to its premature lending, and two played solutions with later results.
Deren is not forced to resume friendship with Vessa after her apology.
Ember can be disappointed by that without making either person comply.

The Perception DC24 door inspection is authored gameplay balance.

All outcomes preserve safety and allow the full friendship.
No roll decides the truth of Ember's values, her affection, or whether she deserves care.

The Trickster branch reveals a possible direction to Anet and then uses a willing ordinary carrier.
