# Independent art review: Areelu v3

Reviewed candidate: `art/candidates/Areelu-v3.png`.

The SHA-256 was verified before visual inspection and again after inspection as `6368DDE950B85F3ACA2AC81494F5AB0E92412DAEA2F426BCD9E6BBDB29B71EAA`.

I inspected the actual v3 image at original detail, read its prompt metadata and the independent v2 review, and compared it with available official Areelu portrait references.

## Verdict

**Pass as an art candidate for the experimental Areelu scene.**

V3 preserves the recognizable identity and deliberately revealing costume that worked in v2, while substantially softening the photographic finish into a painterly CRPG-style illustration.

The character remains an attractive, clearly adult woman with a humanized face, dark hair, crimson eyes, forehead jewel, branching chest mark, prominent horns, bare shoulders, and ornate red-black-and-gold sorceress clothing.

The low neckline and exposed skin remain visible and central to the design; this iteration has not answered the style concern by covering her more.

The laboratory, violet rift, tools, close gaze, and Commander foreground still give the scene a clear narrative setting and intimate adult tension.

## Reference basis and limits

Owlcat's official announcement identifies Areelu as a half-fiend and includes official character art: [The Architect of the Worldwound](https://wrath.owlcat.games/news/33).

The portrait artist's portfolio identifies its image as Areelu's Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous portrait and credits Owlcat Games: [Anna Pavleeva, Areelu Vorlesh](https://annapavleeva.artstation.com/projects/Oo9KOk).

The project does not contain an extracted native Areelu 3D model, a model-linked in-game portrait, or a complete studio art-direction sheet.

The available official portrait is a useful source for palette, face, hair, horns, jewel, chest marking, and painterly finish, but it cannot settle exact model details, animation, or the final game crop.

## Disposition

V3 clears my above-90 art-candidate target and is ready for staging against the route's intended scene.

This does not certify a final in-game asset: portrait crops and installed-game presentation remain separate checks, and I did not inspect either here.

This art review does not establish route readiness or predict any other reviewer's score.
