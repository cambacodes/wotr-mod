# Independent art review: Areelu v5

Reviewed candidate: `art/candidates/Areelu-v5.png`.

Candidate SHA256: `F9A307C695E70478D3099F98652EDD2AA6C883C34BAD1D96DEB57C9E48C0011B`, verified before and after visual inspection.

Reference inspected: `reference/art-review/areelu-official/owlcat-full-portrait.jpg`, SHA256 `4697AEB63A573FAEC09D49903F6BF49EF989858359DD6872B412B546EC4537D6`.

The reference is the full portrait from [Owlcat Games' official Areelu post](https://x.com/OwlcatGames/status/1509138834105245698).

I assessed the actual v5 image at original detail against that full portrait.

## Verdict

V5 addresses both targeted fidelity fixes: the horn bands now read as silver, and the chest mark is smaller and higher toward Areelu's left clavicle.

The mark still glows more brightly and sharply than the subtler violet marking in the official portrait, but its placement now reads as a design echo rather than a different centered emblem.

The red cowl, exposed shoulders, face, pose, and study composition remain intact.

As character art for a face-to-face study or research scene, this is a strong candidate.

The study setting and physical Commander remain a separate assignment constraint, described below rather than counted against the image craft scores.

## Scene assignment

This image shows Areelu and the Commander physically together across an instrument-filled study table.

It does not literally depict a remote projection, and it does not depict the later private meeting in the deliberately empty survey station.

Do not assign it to either of those scenes as a literal illustration.

It can be considered for a separate face-to-face study or research scene.

## Limits and disposition

The supplied full portrait is a two-dimensional reference, not a native model sheet.

I cannot certify exact in-game anatomy, model proportions, materials, animation, or portrait crops from this image comparison.

V5 passes as a strong character-art candidate for an in-person study scene.

The main remaining visual difference is the glow and exact scale of the clavicle mark; reducing its luminosity slightly would bring it closer to the reference.

This review does not approve route quality, runtime use, or final in-game presentation.
