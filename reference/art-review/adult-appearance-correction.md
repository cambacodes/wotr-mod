## Eritrice: council minutes v4

**Scoped appearance verdict: meets the revised adult attractiveness and styled-mane brief.**
Her oval jaw, human nose and lips, mature eyes and composed expression read as an adult woman rather than a cub or an aged lioness.
The golden waved mane and side braid visibly answer the hair requirement.
Violet eyes, rounded ears, tawny fur, claws and the white/red/black/gold collar retain a useful combination of native cues.
The native image has a projecting lion muzzle and no such human hairstyle, so this is deliberate humanization rather than literal likeness.
Short fur on her arms reads as fur, not wrinkled skin; it should not be removed merely to make every exposed surface uniformly smooth.

Shoulders, torso and seated forearms have compatible orientations.
The writing hand grips the quill coherently and the other hand rests on the parchment without an obvious duplicated digit or broken wrist.
The quill reaches the page, but her gaze is turned away from it: this supports a scribe listening during a pause, not proof of actively transcribing that instant.
The desk hides pelvis and legs, which remain unverified rather than passed.

White cloth gathers below the collar and belt, metal fittings have harder highlights, and parchment, wood and fur remain distinguishable.
The fitted chest is pronounced but not visibly broken by impossible fabric tension.
The writing is decorative illegible texture and should not be used to communicate a readable council decision.
All ears and hair are present, although the top margin is modest and unsuitable for an aggressive automatic crop.
No appearance repair is required before a separate exact-page and runtime crop review.

## Eliandra: western arch v2

**Scoped appearance verdict: meets the revised adult attractiveness and hair brief.**
Smooth cheeks, adult facial proportions and dark plum waves with a narrow silver streak produce the requested mature young-adult presentation.
The streak reads as hair color rather than evidence of an elderly face.
Violet eyes and brows, nose and mouth structure, and the purple/gold priestly palette preserve recognizable cues from the native head.
Adding hair changes the native bald silhouette substantially and must remain labeled an authored redesign.

The leaning arm and palm follow the column contact, while the opposite hand closes around the book at a plausible angle.
Shoulders, belt and torso agree, with no obvious reversal of the rib cage or pelvis.
The robe conceals the legs and exits the frame; a full-body anatomy pass is not possible from this image.
The hair has useful breathing room compared with the dragon candidates.

Sleeve folds compress at the elbow and hang below the wrist, while the waist gathers under a separate belt.
Gold edging, book cover, stone and fabric have distinct surfaces; the cloth is opaque rather than inexplicably skin-tight or transparent.
The robe's fine ornament and closures are plausible fantasy ceremonial tailoring, not modern retail styling.
The prominent eight-point stars answer the prompt, but this inspection does not establish an exact canonical Pulura emblem.
She is already standing with a hand on the arch rather than visibly caught in a turn, so that action must be checked against the actual assigned moment.
No facial repair is required; retain the authored-hair disclosure and resolve exact moment and symbol fidelity before assignment.

## Nidalynn: authored humanoid teacher v1

**Scoped appearance verdict: meets the adult face, styled hair and smooth-skin brief; full art acceptance remains held.**
Her face reads as a conventionally attractive adult, with an adult jaw and neck, restrained smile and no childlike eye-to-face proportions.
The silver braid, pale eyes, backward horn forms and localized silver scales carry the silver-dragon reference into a humanized design.
The actual native reference is a dragon head, not this woman's face, ears, hairstyle or clothes.
The notes also identify a separate native human disguise described in a worn dress; this trousered horned form must not be passed off as that verified disguise.

Both arms connect plausibly to the shoulders and reach the basket without a broken torso twist.
The near hand rests among straw and the far hand lifts a small bundle; some fingertips are naturally hidden by straw or the frame.
The seated hips and bent legs are readable, but the feet are cropped and cannot receive a complete anatomy judgment.
The highest horn is entirely present but too close to the top border for a robust portrait crop.

Linen-like shirt creases, a rough wool-like overlayer, heavier trousers, leather belt and woven basket are clearly differentiated.
Seated compression and rolled sleeves behave convincingly, and no modern zipper or synthetic stretch panel is visible.
The shirt collar resembles a simple work shirt; without modern hardware it is not inherently outside the setting.
The comparatively photographic skin, hair, straw and garment detail diverge from the more painted council images.
This is a game-art consistency concern rather than a reason to make her face rougher.
The direct smile toward the viewer and empty basket establish a pleasant care-setting concept, not an observable teaching exchange or proof of rescued eggs.

Required before final selection: add safe horn headroom, settle the intended game-painting treatment, and match the concept to an actual scene without asserting native transformation or egg outcomes.
Do not age her or remove the silver-dragon cues during those repairs.

## Melazmera: authored humanoid negotiator v1

**Scoped appearance verdict: meets the adult face and humanization brief; full art acceptance remains held.**
The mature cheek and mouth structure, swept dark hair, red eyes and cool gray-violet skin read as an attractive adult with a demanding expression.
The skin has subtle surface texture but does not read as cracked, elderly or craggy.
Dark horns and localized overlapping scales preserve draconic identity without retaining a literal dragon muzzle.
Her actual linked portrait is reused BlackDragon art; the candidate notes correctly distinguish that fact from her umbral species and from the unrendered physical model.
Her humanoid face, hair, body, clothing and transformation availability therefore remain authored, not established native facts.

Shoulder, chest, belt and hip directions are coherent.
The open hand has a plausible palm and curled fingers, and the opposite hand makes readable contact with the gemstone surface.
Neither hand shows the gross duplicated anatomy or incompatible wrist direction that would require rejecting the whole pose.
Lower legs and feet are outside the image, so their anatomy is unverified.
The upper horn nearly meets the border, a clear crop-safety weakness even though its tip remains visible.

The garment's metal clasps and purple hanging panels are distinguishable from the dark leather.
The waist and bust shaping is very tight and the trouser surface follows the thighs closely; together with fine creasing, it approaches a molded leather fashion costume rather than visibly substantial practical leather.
That is not proof of impossible construction, but this hard material gate needs either softer documented leather with credible seam allowance or a less uniformly fitted revision.
The large decorated collar and repeated triangular hardware also make this an ornate authored outfit, not evidence of original costume fidelity.
The demanding palm-up gesture is a useful departure from a passive portrait, while the gem, hall and implied bargain remain proposed staging.

Required before final selection: increase horn clearance, improve the visible distinction between flexible and stiff leather panels, and verify an appropriate authored negotiation moment.
Preserve her forceful expression and draconic traits rather than smoothing her into a generic friendly human.

## Decision

All four satisfy the narrow new adult-appearance direction on the viewed pixels.
That conclusion does not approve their entire design, canonical fidelity, source assignment or runtime presentation.
Eritrice and Eliandra can proceed to exact-scene and crop review without another face-generation loop.
Nidalynn and Melazmera need the specified framing and style/material checks before final acceptance.
No numerical scores are awarded here: limited native head evidence, unverified full bodies and unresolved assignment questions do not justify presenting these candidates as having cleared every above-90 production gate.
