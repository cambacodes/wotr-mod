Her gold diadem, center-parted golden hair, luminous eyes and ivory, blue and gold clerical clothing retain the native portrait's identifying combination.
The warmer expression and detailed, attractive adult face fit the current redesign brief.
The painting remains professional and restrained enough for a first personal invitation during working hours.

In the prose she initially holds a print, then puts it on a cloth, examines it, writes a note and eventually rolls it around a tube.
In the portrait her visible hand rests on a closed book.
That difference is acceptable because this assignment uses a posed portrait as the constant visual identity of the speaker, not a literal still of the print examination.
The book is not represented as the deceptive city print, and the image does not display a competing city view or false written title.
The surrounding parchment and counter make sense at her working table without claiming to illustrate the clue that the skill check discovers.
