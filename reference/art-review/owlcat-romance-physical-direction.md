# Owlcat reference and physical scene direction

Date: 2026-09-27.

## Primary reference

Read Owlcat's April 15, 2026 article [How do We Write Romance](https://owlcat.games/news/101), featuring its narrative designers.
Their approach puts the established character and campaign before romance, uses distinct player responses and outcomes, and continues relationship content after its main resolution.
Their examples include a duel, a rose-filled room and optional waterfall bathing, demonstrating romance through events, surroundings and action.
They describe intimacy as revealing a further side of a character without overturning the personality already established.
These are source-supported principles; they do not establish a requirement for interchangeable seductive poses or a native multi-illustration system.

## Project direction from the user's correction

The mod's extra event illustrations are an authored presentation feature.
Their goal is to support the game's embodied interactions and choices, not claim that Owlcat ships the same illustration system.
Keep each character recognizable while allowing natural changes in head angle, expression, posture and camera placement.
Design a sequence around distinct actions and emotional developments before requesting more images.
Make attraction visible when it is present in the scene; do not replace desire, confidence or physical initiative with uniformly reserved posing.
Likewise, anger, calculation, embarrassment or restraint must remain visible when those are the character's actual responses.
Kisses, touch and bodily proximity require the corresponding selected choice and should differ visibly from an earlier conversation.
The customizable Commander may remain outside frame, but that must not erase every gesture or physical consequence from the companion's portrayal.

For Konomi, the currently staged arrival, seated conversation and close portrait are working selections.
The close portrait repeats much of the same head tilt and expression, so its passing standalone review does not prove sufficient sequence variety.
An independent sequence audit now decides which images remain useful and which moments need newly directed action or expression.
Do not label the set final or discard older candidates while this review proceeds.
