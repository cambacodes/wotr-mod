# Independent art review: Areelu v4

Reviewed candidate: `art/candidates/Areelu-v4.png`.

Candidate SHA256: `E6A7B40D583AE670113D05330753574751F7B9C3F54E5D512B41D88DA18D76D7`, verified before and after visual inspection.

Reference inspected: `reference/art-review/areelu-official/owlcat-full-portrait.jpg`, SHA256 `4697AEB63A573FAEC09D49903F6BF49EF989858359DD6872B412B546EC4537D6`.

The reference is from [Owlcat Games' official Areelu portrait post](https://x.com/OwlcatGames/status/1509138834105245698).

I compared the actual v4 image to the full reference, rather than relying on the earlier cropped portrait.

## Verdict

V4 is a strong Areelu character-art candidate, with a revealing costume and conventionally attractive adult humanization that retain her identity.

It corrects much of the v3 costume drift: the central silhouette is again a sleeveless wine-red draped cowl, and the outfit does not read as gold armor.

The main remaining fidelity differences are the brighter, more central chest mark and the warm gold-copper horn bands where the official portrait shows silver-toned bands and a localized mark at the clavicle.

The image depicts Areelu and the Commander physically together across an instrument-filled study table.

It cannot literally illustrate a remote projection or the later private meeting in the deliberately empty survey station.

Keep it for a face-to-face study or research scene if that placement is useful, and select or generate different art for either of those other scenes.

## Reference comparison and limits

V4 restores the official design's deep-red draped sleeveless cowl and keeps the chest open and sensual.

This is closer to the full reference than an armor-heavy interpretation, and the candidate does not hide the costume's exposed shoulders or cleavage.

The official portrait's leather waist, hanging equipment, silver jewelry, horn bands, and expansive wings are only partly visible or differ in V4's close composition.

The most direct revision would be to move the violet mark toward the clavicle and reduce its size and glow, then shift the horn bands toward silver.

The full portrait is a two-dimensional reference, not a native model sheet.

I cannot certify exact 3D proportions, model anatomy, in-game materials, animation, or portrait cropping from these two images.

## Disposition

V4 passes as a strong character-art candidate for an in-person study or research scene.

It does not pass as an illustration of the remote projection or the empty private survey-station meeting.

Do not reuse the study composition for those scenes without changing the scene brief or commissioning a setting-specific image.

If one image must cover multiple scenes, the staging should be described as a stylized character vignette rather than as a literal depiction of either conversation.

This review does not approve the route, runtime use, the native model match, or final in-game cropping.
