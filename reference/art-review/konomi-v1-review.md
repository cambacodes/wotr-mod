## Specific observations

The subtle white cheek fur and orange facial coloring make the humanization legible without reproducing the native elongated muzzle.
Her fine facial texture and slight asymmetry suit the user's clarified preference for recognizable imperfections.
The eyes and mouth carry more personality than an unvarying glamour expression would.
The gold diamonds and red stones closely echo the native garment atlas.
There is no basis here to demand a smoother face or removal of the fox markings.

The resting fingers have plausible overlapping and partly hidden surfaces.
I do not see an obvious fused or duplicated finger that warrants a mandatory hand repair.
Not every finger must be separately exposed in a natural resting pose.
The tail's pale tip is a plausible authored design choice, but this diffuse atlas does not establish the exact native tail texture and should not be cited as proving it.

The office setting is strongest for ordinary capital visits.
It can function as a character portrait beside private narration, but it is not a literal illustration of every private lodging, road scene, letter or ending.
The distant snowy landscape and elaborate citadel furnishings are authored background choices, not verified reconstructions of a particular Drezen room.
Avoid advertising this one image as scene-specific art for the entire route.
