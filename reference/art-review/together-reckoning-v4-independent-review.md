The following judgments concern the delivered pixels.
Exact fiber composition cannot be proven from a painting.

The dress reads as opaque textured woven cloth of moderate weight, with low broad highlights rather than satin glare or plastic shine.

The lamp warms the inward-facing edges and exposed forearms while recesses retain deeper red shadows.

The main dress, pale shirt, navy coat, skin and wooden table remain materially distinguishable.
