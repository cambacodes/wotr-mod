# Reassessment of selected and previously approved art

The latest user instruction reopens the full design review of all settled images.
Do not treat earlier scores as approval under the new requirement.
Keep original files and iteration history; replacing a selected image does not require deleting the previous work.

## Required evidence per selected image

- Exact candidate, staged image and crop hashes, with source-reference provenance.
- Direct native comparison of face, racial cues, hairstyle, age presentation and characteristic expression.
- Embodied persona and racial identity through gaze, posture, gesture and presentation, with the actual page's emotion preserved.
- Sequence-level variation in head angle, pose, camera, expression and physical action, with a narrative reason for each image change.
- Character-specific adult attraction and bodily expression where the scene calls for them; a repeated smile or tighter crop alone does not pass as a new dramatic illustration.
- Bust and body-silhouette comparison where the source actually shows them; explicitly unresolved where it does not.
- Clothing, jewelry and exposed-skin comparison, including date outfits, transformations and historical state.
- Scene-appropriate civilian or private wardrobe rather than automatic reuse of combat armor, trousers, scarf and guards.
- Meaningful new outfit design when requested: distinct silhouette, garment type, cut, drape or color arrangement, not merely fabric replacement, accessory removal or simplified equipment.
- Setting-appropriate fabric, seams, closures and tailoring, rejecting accidental modern or mass-produced styling while respecting the character's resources and culture.
- Visible material differentiation: weave, weight, stiffness, roughness, sheen and thickness, not a uniform cloth texture.
- Lighting and opacity consistent with the selected material, including transmitted light, layering, lining and dense seams where relevant.
- Physical drape, tension, compression and contact consistent with garment cut, gravity and pose rather than uniform clinging or shapeless cloth.
- Conventional attractiveness without genericizing the character or erasing identifying imperfections.
- Actual page, setting, costume and participant compatibility, followed by crop and runtime checks.

Each applicable review dimension must independently clear the required threshold.
An author cannot approve their own image, and flattering overall scores cannot override a wardrobe or identity failure.
Native-fidelity, aesthetic/anatomy and scene-continuity reviews must address their own evidence rather than reuse an old average.
These are review tasks, not guaranteed scores.
