The outfit matters to this result.

The expression retains amusement and calculation rather than replacing Nurah with a uniformly innocent or submissive presentation.

Feet and the native ankle ornament are outside the candidate's lower crop, so this review makes no claim about their rendering.
The framing is suitable as the source painting; required runtime aspect ratios and small portrait readability have not been inspected.
Any story assignment must still distinguish a reusable identity portrait from a literal scene illustration.
