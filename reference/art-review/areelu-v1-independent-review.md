# Independent art review: Areelu v1

Reviewed image: `art/candidates/Areelu-v1.png`.

## Verdict

**Promising draft, not approved as an in-game Areelu portrait or scene illustration.**

It is a polished, attractive, adult-coded half-demon sorceress scene with a convincing laboratory setting and a strong intimate composition.

Its largest weakness is identity fidelity: the image reads as a generic silver-haired amber-eyed horned sorceress rather than a clearly recognizable Areelu.

The brief requires retaining the character's defining design, and the candidate changes several high-value visual markers while leaning into a glamour portrait style that differs from the more graphic, painterly Areelu art shared by Owlcat.

The project folder contains no native Areelu model export, native portrait file, or exact art-direction sheet for side-by-side model verification.

For a limited visual cross-check, Owlcat's account shared an Areelu portrait at [Owlcat Games' Areelu portrait post](https://x.com/OwlcatGames/status/1510995609574121473).

That portrait shows a strong dark-purple palette, dark hair, vivid crimson eyes, red forehead jewelry, and a red chest mark.

It is promotional character art, not proof of every detail in the game's 3D model.

The visual ratings below are independent estimates for this image only and do not predict acceptance or future reviewer scores.

## Specific observations

The character remains immediately legible as a horned, powerful fantasy woman, and the composition clearly establishes that she is facing the Commander across a worktable.

The scene props support an arcane researcher: shelves, instruments, books, candles, and a violet phenomenon outside the window give her setting a purposeful identity.

The foreground figure helps connect the art to the romance route rather than presenting a detached character pin-up.

The large curved black horns are visually strong, but the image does not show wings, a tail, or the distinctive red marks and jewelry present in the available Areelu reference.

The silver hair and golden eyes are the most damaging identity changes because they dominate the face, the focal point of the composition.

The low-cut strapped bodice emphasizes cleavage and exposed shoulders, while the red wrap and dark sleeves keep the outfit from becoming anachronistically minimal.

The rendering is attractive and clean, but its realistic skin detail, lens-like lighting, and photographic depth look more like a cinematic fan-art still than the game's stylized painted character art.

## Revision direction

Keep the scene layout, mature attractiveness, commanding horns, red wrap, and warm-cool laboratory lighting.

Bring the face closer to Areelu by restoring dark hair, saturated crimson eyes, her red forehead ornament, and the visible chest mark or another recognizable red magical accent.

Make the garment read as her layered dark and crimson sorceress attire, preserving a sensual neckline while reducing the generic strapped-corset look.

Make her expression more appraising and self-possessed, with the intimacy coming from sustained eye contact and deliberate proximity instead of an overtly coy pose.

If this is intended for the dialogue portrait system, create and review the necessary portrait crops separately; this review covers only the full 1536 by 1024 source composition.

## Limits and final disposition

I did not inspect a local native Areelu model, exact in-game portrait, final in-game crop, or installed game rendering.

The project brief and canon notes establish Areelu's identity and character themes but do not include an authoritative visual model sheet.

This is a strong visual draft with mature appeal and a fitting setting, but the current candidate needs a recognizable design pass before it can be treated as an Areelu asset.
