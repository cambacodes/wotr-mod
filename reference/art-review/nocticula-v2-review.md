## What actually improved

V1's large horns rose and curled like ram horns.
V2 replaces that dominant silhouette with a wide, swept arrangement closer to the native design, with smaller horns behind the crown.
The pose and formal clothing remain coherent after that change.

V1 had distinct lavender irises and dark pupils.
V2's eyes are largely uniform and luminous, restoring an important supernatural cue.
Their glow is brighter and more violet than the comparatively pale native eyes, but does not obscure their shape or the expression.

The change in rendering is visible, especially in the sky, distant buildings, stone, wing membranes, and fabric.
It is not solely a color adjustment.
The face still has softer transitions than the native game's angular portrait, but it belongs to the same illustration rather than looking pasted onto the painted background.

## Remaining weaknesses and practical restrictions

The resemblance relies on the combined supernatural features more than on matching the original face.
Long flowing hair replaces the shorter, straighter native silhouette, and the high collar hides the chest markings.
Those differences are acceptable under the current attractive-redesign brief, but they should be acknowledged when showing this candidate to the user.
Do not describe it as an exact native likeness.

If another revision is made, the best refinement is stronger painted facial planes and fewer individually rendered hair strands.
A slightly less intense eye glow would also move it closer to the native reference.
I do not require another generation solely for these refinements under this scoped review.

The horns have little horizontal spare space.
A standard portrait crop may cut them or force the face to become too small.
I inspected the delivered full image and face within that image; I did not generate or evaluate the actual portrait-size crop set or render it in the game UI.
Crop review remains necessary before runtime acceptance.

The high-collared gown and harbor are authored styling, not evidence of a particular native outfit, location visit, or quest event.
Use the image only where that styling does not contradict the actual scene.
An art pass does not establish that Nocticula is affectionate, agreeable, redeemed, or romantically available in the accompanying writing.
Her intimidating composure here is compatible with negotiation or self-interested attention.

No staging or assignment was performed by this reviewer.
