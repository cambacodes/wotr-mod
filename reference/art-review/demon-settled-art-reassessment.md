## Chivarro v4

The adult face is attractive through its softly modeled lower face, asymmetric smile and smooth eyeless upper face.
The design preserves warm blond hair, pointed ears, dark banded horns, gold jewelry and lilitu recognition.
The native thumbnail has more apparent closed-lid relief, while v4 has a smoother continuous surface; that is an intentional attractive interpretation of eyeless anatomy, not restored human eyes.
Its warm fair skin cannot be certified as an exact model color from the thumbnail's lighting.
The copper dress has a low neckline, bare shoulders, loose hanging sleeves and a leg opening.
It has visible adult sensuality and is not a blanket conservative cover-up.
Its full bust and shaped waist are coherent, but the reference contains no usable bust or waist evidence against which to certify their size.
The elaborate textile, tailoring and furniture are authored, not demonstrated native costume details.
The visible hands and grasped fabric are coherent at this resolution.

The native evidence brief cites a simple revealing dress; this rich copper garment should remain a specific chosen outfit rather than being presented as an exact default costume recreation.
The single exported `minachiv.the_entrance_she_wants/warm` page explicitly changes her dress, describes a loose sleeve and cup, and rehearses her welcome beside a window.
The image supports that general setting and dress choice.
It does not literally show her catching a cuff just before it touches a cup, so it should be treated as an illustration of the selected outfit rather than a frame-accurate action shot.
No comparative promise that she is more beautiful than Minagho is warranted; both meet this reviewer's facial-appeal threshold independently.

## Minagho v2

The candidate closely preserves the native portrait's long pale hair, lavender-pink skin, huge ridged curling horns, gold bands, elongated ears, hoop earrings, smooth eyeless upper face and red lips.
The amused tilted head and controlled smile fit the recognizable expression rather than merely supplying an attractive generic demon.
Both horns have adequate frame margin.
The exposed shoulders and upper chest, high ornamental throat piece, separate long red sleeves, red-and-gold fitted dress and prominent red stones closely follow the visible native design.
The upper-torso silhouette remains full and fitted; no material flattening or raised neckline is apparent.
Pose, lighting and perspective prevent an exact numerical comparison of bust volume.
The skirt below the native crop is an authored extrapolation, not confirmed original model evidence.
Hands, long nails, letter grip and seated proportions are coherent.

The forehead is unmarked in both inspected paintings.
That does not prove the brand is absent in every narrative history.
The retained native brief distinguishes eyeless anatomy from Baphomet's brand and cites the parent's blood trick as one specific release history.
Brand removal does not restore eyes, and this candidate correctly remains eyeless.
Before assignment, require the actual applicable brand-resolved history, or supply an appropriate marked variant for histories still carrying it.
Do not substitute this true-form image for the Mina disguise.
The inspected `minachiv.two_answers` export requires `minagho.ran_complete` and `minagho.book_three_finished`, but this visual review did not prove that these conditions imply an absent brand across every permitted parent history.
That implication remains a verification task.

## Jerribeth v4

The image supplies an attractive adult humanlike face with pale lilac-gray skin, cyan-blue eyes, a raised teal crest, scaled side structures, dark lateral horns and large veined wings.
These anchors retain a recognizable connection to the native oolioddroo image despite substantial intentional humanization.
The smaller human eyes and ordinary lips replace enormous luminous insect eyes and the proboscis.
This meets the user's permission to humanize the design; it is not literal native facial morphology and must not be described as such.
The expression remains cool and assessing.
The crossed hands, long fingers and wing silhouettes are visually coherent, though wing roots disappear behind the shoulders and cannot establish exact anatomy.

The high-necked, fully covered plum-and-dark layered gown is an authored costume.
The thumbnail shows no body or clothing sufficient to establish this gown, its bust construction, its coverage or its four-wing arrangement as a native match.
A visible fitted bust in the candidate does not resolve that evidential gap.
Neither enlarging breasts nor removing clothing would fix missing reference evidence by itself.
Obtain a full model render or further visual evidence before claiming native wardrobe fidelity, and keep any chosen alternate gown explicitly scene-specific.

There is a reproduced form-assignment defect independent of that missing evidence.
`jerribeth.guise/start` explicitly presents a slim elven figure with a shimmer; `jerribeth.guise/guise` keeps that face for the evening.
Both explicitly assign `Portrait: Jerribeth`, which resolves to the winged, horned v4.
`jerribeth.guise/true` also assigns the same portrait when the elven outline falls away.
Thus the story's offered form choice has no corresponding visual transition.
Use a separately reviewed authored elven-guise asset on the guise branch and the appropriate true-form image when she changes back.
The native-unit search evidence does not establish a native elven raster, so that guise must remain labeled authored.
Do not relabel v4 itself as an elf or claim the humanoid face alone solves the distinction.

## Nurah v1

The candidate keeps the native blonde side braid, dark blue hairband, hazel-green eyes, pointed ears, assessing smile and warm fair complexion.
Her face has adult proportions and subtle natural texture; it does not read as a child or a heavily aged redesign.
The native rose sleeves, teal bodice with blue patterned center, dark shoulder cape, studded forearm guards, blue sash, belt and pouch are closely retained.
The neckline and upper-chest exposure follow the original practical travel outfit rather than imposing new cleavage or added prudish coverage.
Her compact figure, visible fitted torso and comfortable seated posture retain the intended small adult stature, although no independent height scale can be measured from the furniture.
Hands on the notebook and desk are coherent.
The lower legs and feet are cropped out, so native bare-foot treatment is unassessed, not verified.
The writing-room setting is authored and suits a neutral speaker portrait, but must not automatically illustrate prison, outdoor, hooded or disguise scenes.
The candidate is recognizably Nurah rather than a generic miniature human.
