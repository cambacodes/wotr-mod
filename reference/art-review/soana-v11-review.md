## What changed in the face

V10 has pronounced folds beside the mouth, a more furrowed brow, tighter lips and stronger shadows under the cheekbones.
V11 fills and softens those areas enough to change the overall impression immediately.
The silver hair remains a distinctive color rather than a cue requiring an elderly face.
Some natural shading and small expression lines remain, which the user permits.
The lower cheek and neck no longer look weathered or sinewy.
The result retains the broad facial structure needed for her dwarf identity.

The native portrait supports the markings, hair direction, brown eyes and general facial proportions.
It does not require the rejected wrinkled treatment.
The younger adult appearance is an authored visual redesign and makes no claim about her canonical age or biography.

## Remaining action-specific limitation

The pale strip from her working hand descends diagonally and curls into the crowded upper rim near a front upright.

The image does not make their connection sufficiently visible to establish that the hand-held strip is replacing that lower row.
The open gap improves the suggestion of repair, but the complete path still depends on reading the prompt into the picture.
