## Facial appeal and eyeless anatomy

The two oval eyelid-like bulges have gone.
Soft shading now connects the forehead, nose bridge and upper cheeks without a pair of closed-eye outlines.
There are no visible lashes, irises, wounds or empty sockets.
This reads as an intentionally eyeless lilitu, rather than a woman closing her eyes.
That change is evident at the full illustration size and directly addresses the earlier blocker.

The lower face also looks softer and more inviting.
The lifted mouth corner, gently curved lips and cheek contour give her a restrained amused expression that is easier to read than v2's harder face.
The nose highlight is less abrupt, and the transition toward the chin is more graceful.
I find this a real improvement in adult facial attractiveness, not merely a lighting variation.

The uninterrupted upper face necessarily remains uncanny.
It is still quite smooth, and someone who dislikes eyeless anatomy may continue to find it mannequin-like.
In this version, the mouth's asymmetry and the modeled nose and cheeks supply enough expression and structure that I do not consider the face blank or unfinished.
I would keep that restrained expression rather than add human eyes to solve the remaining subjective discomfort.

Minagho-v2 still has a more theatrical expression and a closer composition that gives her face more attention.
V4 does not erase that difference, and its smile remains subtler than Minagho's.
Chivarro now looks appealing on her own terms while retaining her composed hostess bearing.
She has not become a copy of Minagho.

## Identity and execution

Warm blond hair, gold-banded dark horns, pointed ears, red earrings, gold jewelry and the copper gown preserve the established Chivarro design.
The native generic lilitu reference supports her broad racial traits and poised presentation, not a uniquely canonical Chivarro face or this costume.
The smoother upper face follows the native textual eyeless identity while intentionally departing from the thumbnail's raised oval forms.

She reads clearly as an adult.
The softer cheeks and chin do not make her look adolescent, and there is no added elderly or jagged treatment.
I see no new obvious hand, limb, horn or clothing defect.
The light remains consistent with the window, and the painterly face belongs with the room and garment rendering.
Some shading appears softer than in earlier versions, but I see no distracting seam around the edited face.

The full-body framing, horn headroom, pose, loose sleeve, unused dark gown, hanging cloth, cup and box remain visually consistent with the earlier composition.
This is not a claim of exact pixel preservation outside the face.
