## Authored humanoid design

The planned attractive adult humanoid presentation is an authored alternate form.
Current manuscript passages still describe a dragon with foreclaws and a scaled jaw; they do not justify assigning humanoid art without an explicit supported transformation.
That design must be labeled as an alternate form, not a recovered native human portrait.
Use the rendered dragon reference to choose recognizable horns, scale accents, coloring and eye treatment before generation.
Retain an attractive adult face and human proportions while making her draconic identity clear.
Her posture and expression should preserve pride, territorial confidence and capacity for violence.
A softer romantic expression belongs to a particular earned moment, not every portrait.
Clothing in the authored form should follow the actual scene and her chosen presentation; no native human outfit has been established by these sources.

The brood-saved and lost-clutch histories must remain distinct.
Do not depict living offspring in a lost-clutch branch or imply that romance restores them.
Do not depict a durable main-campaign body merely because a DLC conversation exists.
Physical presence, transformation and recovery need the route's acquisition conditions before their images are assigned.
