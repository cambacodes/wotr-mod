# Independent art review: Areelu v2

Reviewed candidate: `art/candidates/Areelu-v2.png`.

The SHA-256 was verified before visual inspection and again after inspection as `1A41B9D08515F4AE6B7FE963D94D967772743C6BE2C7C2011E97F92A3BF1BB9E`.

I read the current `art/CHARACTER-DESIGN.md`, the v2 prompt metadata and the v1 review for revision context, then assessed the actual v2 image independently.

The image is 1536 by 1024 pixels.

## Verdict

**Fail for final art approval; pass as a strong scene-art candidate for another iteration.**

This revision solves most of v1's identity problems and reads clearly as Areelu: the image restores her dark hair, vivid red eyes, red forehead jewel and chest marking, and pairs them with an imposing horned silhouette and ornate red, black and gold clothing.

The humanized face is conventionally attractive and adult-coded without making her look generic or erasing the half-fiend cues.

The study, magical instruments, violet rift, and Commander in the foreground make the image specific to a private meeting in the route's arcane-research setting.

The remaining gate is presentation fidelity.

The image is polished cinematic digital realism with strong photographic depth and glossy lighting, while the available official portrait is painterly.

That difference is conspicuous enough that I would not yet approve it as final game-facing scene art intended to sit beside native assets.

## Comparison and limits

The official Owlcat announcement identifies Areelu as the half-fiend architect of the Worldwound and includes official character art: [The Architect of the Worldwound](https://wrath.owlcat.games/news/33).

The portrait artist's portfolio identifies the image as the Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous portrait and credits Owlcat Games: [Anna Pavleeva, Areelu Vorlesh](https://annapavleeva.artstation.com/projects/Oo9KOk).

Those sources support the character identity and provide a reliable portrait-style reference, but they are not a native 3D model sheet.

The project contains no extracted native Areelu model, model-linked portrait, or exact game art-direction sheet for direct comparison.

The previous v1 review documented that limitation; I did not treat its scores as evidence for this candidate.

## Revision direction

Keep the v2 identity design, attractive adult face, ornate revealing costume, direct gaze, Commander framing, laboratory objects, and purple rift.

The next iteration should move the surface finish toward the painterly official portrait through softer brush-like edges and less photographic skin and depth treatment, while preserving the composition and mature sensuality.

If selected for implementation, portrait crops and the installed game's actual dialogue presentation still need separate review.

## Disposition

This is a substantial improvement over v1 and is ready for an art-direction decision as a scene-art candidate.

It does not pass final art approval because the game-style-fit score remains below the project's above-90 target and the native model has not been inspected.

No conclusion about route readiness follows from this art review.
