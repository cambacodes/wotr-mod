## Why `talk` needs a different framing

There are distinct actual incoming paths.
The player can arrive directly from `lovers`, `missed_lovers` or `new` by choosing company without the offered closer action.
The player can arrive from `near` after choosing to remain close without kissing.
Both `kiss` responses also lead to `talk` and set the kissed flag.

Nothing at `talk` resets the chair distance or says their hands have returned to Konomi's lap and separate armrest.
Full seated v4 would therefore select a particular visible posture for all three histories without textual support.
On a quiet incoming path it could fit individually, but the current single exported node does not select a different portrait by incoming action.
Do not call that a universal assignment pass.
There is no need to change those choices merely to accommodate an existing image.

## Proposed economical art coverage

A close conversational image could cover `talk`, `letters` and `others` while retaining the private warmth of v4.
Frame her complete ears, face, hair and identifiable upper gown, with a small amount of consistent warm room background.
Leave both hands, lap, knees and the relative chair distance outside the frame.
That would neither assert contact on a declined branch nor show her hands elsewhere while the text describes touching or resting nearby.
It would function as a scene-consistent conversational portrait rather than pretending to illustrate an invisible hand gesture.

The expression should be attentive and personally engaged, not blank or emotionally neutral merely because the physical action is offscreen.
The proposition does not require covering the neckline, reducing the bust or replacing the gown.
Keep the already reviewed adult design, materials and setting.
The same framing might also serve `lovers` and `missed_lovers` if its expression can accommodate the differing history without depicting an unchosen hand clasp.
That suitability must be judged on the actual delivered image or crop.

A crop of seated v4 is a possible starting point, but a crop has not been produced or inspected here.
It must retain full ear outlines and adequate detail when displayed at the book-event size.
Do not assume enlargement of a small face region will preserve the current finish.
A newly generated close composition is another option, not an automatic requirement.

`near` and `kiss` are the stronger candidates for dedicated milestone illustrations.
For `near`, a close Konomi pose could show her reaching beyond the frame and leaning into the selected shared space without fixing the Commander's appearance.
For `kiss`, the text includes a brief pullback while her hand remains at the neck/collar; an appropriate close aftermath composition could depict that exact moment without inventing a Commander face or body type.
These are design briefs only.
Neither hypothetical image receives approval or a score in this review.
Do not use a neutral portrait as a claim that the requested milestone art has been completed.
