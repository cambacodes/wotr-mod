These are judgments of this revision after viewing it, not predictions or scores for the route.

The native reference has darker, more open-looking empty sockets.
The candidate softens them into healed, recessed scarred surfaces that can also read as closed damaged lids.
They remain sufficiently distinct from ordinary closed eyes, and nothing depicts restored sight.
I accept that treatment under the current attractive redesign brief.
Removing the scars or replacing them with ordinary seeing eyes would lose an essential character cue.

The longer braid and expanded costume are authored design choices rather than exact copies of the small native crop.
The recognizable fringe, cheek paint and chin mark do more useful identity work than matching every strand of hair.
The image preserves those details clearly.

The snowy view and shaped decorative carving are authored setting and prop details.
The image can support a general workshop portrait, but it does not substantiate an exact native season, a permanent new residence, or the particular inherited patterns in the later Sella scene.
Its tactile pose fits Gesmerha's blindness without requiring that she visually inspect the carving.
The carving's birdlike outline is legible enough as a handmade decorative object and does not need a precise species attribution.
