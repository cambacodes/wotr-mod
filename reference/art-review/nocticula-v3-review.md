## Costume comparison

The native portrait makes exposed skin and large dark markings part of Nocticula's design.
V2 concealed that structure beneath a gown and therefore failed the user's later explicit requirement, regardless of its other attractive features.
V3 visibly restores the required shoulders, upper arms, and marked abdomen.
There is no added jacket, sleeve, high collar, or shawl replacing those features.
The neckline is open, and the armor covers the breasts without turning into a full torso covering.
The relaxed hands on the book preserve a neutral portrait rather than introducing a sexual action or pose.

The result is still a redesign, not an exact costume reproduction.
Its curling bronze breastplate and bracers are heavier and more elaborate than the native portrait's sparse chains and ornaments.
Its markings use a more symmetrical pattern and do not reproduce every native shape.
Those differences do not defeat the revised brief's required silhouette and exposed-skin pattern, but should remain visible qualifications when presenting the candidate.
The lower hips are mostly obscured by the table and book, so this review cannot establish a complete full-body costume match.

## Identity and remaining refinements

Shortening the hair helps considerably.
It now frames the face near the shoulders instead of covering much of the torso with long flowing strands.
The native portrait has an even straighter, cleaner hair silhouette, but v3 retains enough of that cue to support recognition.
The facial expression remains aloof and appraising rather than cheerfully agreeable.

The eyes glow more brightly and more violet than the pale native eyes.
The horns retain the corrected lateral spread, although their sculpted ridges differ from the flatter native rendering.
Neither difference requires another generation under this scoped review.
If refined further, lower the eye glow slightly and simplify the hair and facial highlights toward the game's painted planes.

The viewer-left outer horn remains close to the image boundary.
Do not infer that a smaller portrait crop will preserve the horn silhouette or enough of the costume simply because the full image passes.
I inspected the full delivered image; I did not evaluate an exported portrait-size crop set or its rendering inside the game.
Those are separate remaining checks.

No art was staged and no story scene was assigned by this reviewer.
