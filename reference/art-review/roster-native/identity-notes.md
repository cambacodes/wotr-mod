# Native portrait identity notes

These notes follow direct inspection of all eleven unit-linked extracted portraits in this directory.
`provenance.json` records unit, portrait and texture links, dimensions and hashes.
`tools/extract-roster-art-references.py` reproduces extraction from the installed game with the isolated RanRomanceTools UnityPy environment.
The files are native reference images, not generated candidates or approved replacement portraits.
The three requested sizes can resolve to the same small texture and must not be counted as three independent design references.
No linked prefab has been rendered for this inspection.

## Anevia and Irabeth

The capital unit references resolve to full native portraits rather than only generated initiative thumbnails.
Anevia's native portrait shows short dark side-swept hair, a blue-and-gold checked scarf, pale shirt, fitted brown leather armor, bracers, bow, quiver and sword.
Irabeth's native portrait shows green skin, pointed ears, small lower tusks, short blue-black hair, gold plate armor with paired silver emblems, sword and crossbow.
The armor and crossed-arm poses limit what can be established about underlying bust anatomy.
Compare the visible clothing silhouette directly; do not claim unsupported measurements beneath armor.
The staged individual portraits and shared image have been handed to an independent reviewer under the latest full-design requirement.
Native references do not prove that an armored shared portrait suits every private scene.

## Vellexia

The inspected variants share light skin, red eyes, dark upright ridged horns, pointed ears, red lips and blond hair gathered into a high knot.
Her default and first-date thumbnails show the same green-and-gold collar treatment.
The second-date thumbnail has a broad gold circlet and dark high collar.
The third-date thumbnail has a patterned dark red-and-gold high collar without that circlet.
An authored attractive portrait should preserve her particular face and deliberate aristocratic bearing.
Do not assume a revealing torso costume from her race; these references show the head and high neck clothing only.
Full-body interpretation needs model evidence or an explicitly authored outfit.

## Shamira

The inspected portrait shows dark warm skin, luminous green eyes, orange hair, dark upright horns, pointed ears and a broad gold headband.
There is bright wing-like light behind the figure, but the thumbnail does not establish the full wing shape or rendering effects.
The narrow crop supplies collar and shoulder ornaments, not a complete costume.
Preserve the strong, severe expression in an attractive humanized design.
Do not replace her coloring and headwear with Nocticula's or a generic succubus palette.

## Herrax

The linked Herraxa portrait shows red side-swept hair, red eyes, dark horns, pointed ears and multiple facial scars.
Both eyes appear luminous in this thumbnail.
That visual observation alone cannot settle any dialogue claim about eye damage or restoration.
Keep the scars that establish recognition while using appealing facial proportions and softer rendering where appropriate.
The thumbnail shows neck ornaments and exposed shoulders, with insufficient coverage to infer a complete outfit or the condition of her wings.

## Eliandra

The portrait shows a bald woman with warm light skin, luminous pale eyes, purple eye makeup, a purple collar and gold ornaments.
The source does not support adding generic flowing hair as a default identity cue.
An attractive redesign can preserve the bald head, expressive eyes and distinctive purple-and-gold treatment.
Full costume, age detail and location still need additional evidence and scene context.

## Yaniel

Both inspected records show short gray hair falling across one side of the forehead, pointed ears and long facial marks.
The rescued-state thumbnail has darkened eye sockets, a thinner-looking face and little visible clothing detail.
The Midnight Fane thumbnail shows a healthier-looking face and visible chainmail at the neck.
These are distinct unit-linked references, not proof that a particular recovery scene has already occurred in the mod.
Match the selected appearance to the actual chronology and preserve enough of the gray hair, ears and facial marks to identify the real Yaniel.
Neither reference is the Areelu-disguise portrait.

## Review boundary

These observations guide future art briefs and do not grant visual or runtime approval.
A generated image still needs independent recognition, attractiveness, anatomy, costume, setting and actual page-assignment review.
