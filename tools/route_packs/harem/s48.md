   The augmented verification export registers the row explicitly in memory.
2. `Rules.ParticipantsAvailable` and its Python mirror demand harem eligibility
   even for private protected dockets. This wrongly adds a romance prerequisite
   to the sheet's independent channel. The row adds no such requirement itself;
   a regression test records the current failure rather than certifying it.

   Paid answers therefore remain terminals. The postselection display specified
   by GLOBAL-TC needs a shared consequence-cue facility without another scene
   completion. `s48.paid_text()` supplies that proposed cue's non-explicit text;
   the implemented historical Ledger reader records both delivered concessions.
4. Herrax's pre-existing unfinished-contract discovery/courier readers still
   need the sheet's settled variant at their route-owned hosts. This row does
   not edit the other route's source or add extra delivery scenes to fake it.
5. K3 packet assembly and the integrated census belong to the coordinator.
   Its child must reuse S48 reply flags and its shared completion veto.
6. The strict verifier classifies `notice.dismissed` as a bodily departure by
   suffix, yielding P1 errors for all four notice variants. Preserve the sheet's
   exact witness. Shared `storylines/earned_presence.py::DEPARTURE_EXEMPTIONS`
   needs the entry `("household", "household.pair.herrax_minagho.notice.dismissed")`
   with reason: "Dismisses only the S48 negotiation; neither woman departs and
   no romance closes." The checker reads that shared Python dictionary, not
   row-local serialized metadata. Adding it to household unavailability would
   incorrectly disable unrelated household content and was not implemented.
