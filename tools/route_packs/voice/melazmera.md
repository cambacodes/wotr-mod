## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** everything: sailors, dead souls, a demon queen. She judges the world by taste ("salty").
- **Craves:** her hoard, real and illusory, and above all its *owning*. A thief is something to eat or to keep.
- **Agenda:** eat, hoard, never be robbed, and win every exchange. The Commander stole from her; now she owns the Commander.
- **Strengths:** shadow breath (`17c304c0`); illusions over rocks; scales no sailor's weapon pierces (`2433842e`).
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Distracted by noise and chains (`7580716e`).
  - A hook can turn her in the air (`784e7903`).
  - Her own greed, which makes her bargainable.
  - She has never been given something for nothing and cannot bear owing (`melazmera.trickster.beat.putting_down:coin`).

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty:** appetite without malice. That is worse than malice. She is delighted while she eats, purring and giggling.
- **Humiliation:** delivered through the swindle. She pays in illusion-gold under the Commander's seal, and the gold turns to rock past the quay, leaving the debt on the Commander's name: "You said pay them. You did not say in what."
- **Desire:** possessive and hoard-shaped. The Commander is a treasure that talks back.
- **Power:** size, breath and teeth. Concessions are trades she wins: spare the captain, and take his ship, his arm or his story.

## Mouth
- **Register:** childish-gluttonous with a predator's grin. Simple sentences, repetition, delighted cruelty, "thief" as a pet name. An occasional "bitch", saved for rivals ("the horned one"). Keep the giggle (canon, `70c883b1`).
- **Target:** low profanity, high appetite: one food image per scene and one threat of eating.
- **Example register:** "He was crunchy. You wanted him alive? You should have said so before he was crunchy."

## She never says
- "I will not eat them, because you did not want me to." Every concession carries a price or a loophole. Every conceding "Very well" in the route needs one.
- Receipts, factors, payment ledgers or recipients' marks.
- Sorry about sailors. "I was hungry, and they were there, and they were salty" is her ceiling.
- Anything moral about restitution. If she pays, she swindles.
- Cute drift: candle-crunching, capitals, voice imitation. She is not a pet.

## Canon guardrails
- **Range:** Colyphyr, its skies and the airship crossing (AirAdventures); a lair of illusion-wrapped rocks over dull-looking treasure.
- **Hepzamirah:** a canon truce (`a68b28c8`) with unstated terms.

## DO NOT INVENT
- The "fat slave every new moon" tribute (mod-invented, user ruling). Use only "a truce" (`a68b28c8`). If payment is mentioned, keep it vague and canon-safe; do not specify slaves.
- "Kerz's crew". Write "the captain's crew".
- Her origin, age or a dragon mother; extensions of the "drowned king" or her woman form.
- Presence anywhere native scenes would contradict.

## Falling for the Commander
She falls the way a dragon does: she puts the Commander in the hoard, and nothing in the hoard leaves.

What she does:
- Brings the Commander shiny things and tests whether the Commander can tell the real from the illusion.
- Spares what the Commander forbids, on the letter, and eats what was left unsaid (the cows).
- Says "I should eat you" and finds it keeps not happening (`melazmera.trickster.beat.crew:me`). That tension never resolves into safety.

What does not change:
- Her ending keeps eating thieves.
- Her epilogue restates appetite and possession.
- She does not soften. She hoards.
