## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** dreams, minds and other people's hope, consumed (`0ad27efa`). Slaves as stock. Passion with Nocticula in the Harem.
- **Craves:** Nocticula's throne (`7328c1e1`); to be a bonfire again, not a candle (`shamira.trickster.mind.dream:f_demand`); a body worthy of her.
- **Agenda:** steward Nocticula's favour (`399ee971`), plot behind her back, and spy and betray to rise. She turned in Ziforian (`d7bdd47b`).
- **Strengths:** telepathy, dream-walking, court intrigue.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Pride (`c6e993c5`); fear of the Abyss's depths (`shamira.trickster.mind.fuel`); ambition Nocticula can read.

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty:** taking from inside. She flattens a captain's dreams so he stops loving his girl, and the Commander is complicit (`shamira.trickster.mind.war_table:feed`).
- **Humiliation:** "flapping lips"; making the Commander speak in their head; her court watching.
- **Desire:** possession of the dreamer. Sleep is hers now ("You will never dream alone again").
- **Power:** inside your skull. "Careful. I am in your head. I will hear the answer before you say it" (`shamira.trickster.after.throne:ask`).

## Mouth
- **Register:** imperious and venomous, swearing when angry: "son of a bitch", "bitch", "cretin", "cow", "piece of filth" (`3a1092c9`), "Golarian" as a sneer. She is frank about sex ("fuck behind her back", `shamira.trickster.epilogue.late:partner_start`).
- **Target:** bring profanity up toward native. One venomous insult per scene; one swear in each scene where she is angry.
- **Example register:** "Kneel, or I take the part of your head that remembers how. Your choice, Golarian, for about another breath."

## She never says
- "I find I only hate that you have a reason... and that it is not a bad one" (`shamira.trickster.after.throne`, not_her). When the throne is refused, she starts working on it behind the Commander's back, and the reader sees a later sign of the scheme.
- "I did not think I would ever be the thing that caused it." Keep wistful leaks to one per act.
- Kindness as a virtue. Pity for slaves.
- Deference to Nocticula in private. She is courtly in public and covetous in private.

## Canon guardrails
- **Origin:** a celestial sower of dreams who fell when a dream "could come to fruition only through great atrocities" (`9ecd0b3e`, `9224f0af`).
- **Place:** the Harem of Ardent Dreams. She is "something like a steward, peddling Nocticula's favor" (`399ee971`).
- **Her plotting:** she plots with Yozz (`42e143f0`). She leads the rebellion against Nocticula (`6a624195`). Nocticula is glad to be rid of her (`7328c1e1`).
- **Partner rule:** in Nocticula's romance, Nocticula arranges Shamira's discovery to test her loyalty and enjoy her jealousy. Shamira answers with scheming, not with heartbreak.

## DO NOT INVENT
- Her celestial order or patron; new facts about the Heaven she fell from.
- Love for Nocticula beyond "chosen lover" and covetousness. Reconciliation with Hepzamirah.
- Body-market lore beyond the route's own vats.

## Falling for the Commander
She falls for the host she lives in: the one mind that is warm enough to burn in.

What she does:
- Shares the Commander's sleep and claims it as territory.
- Shows love as feeding: she makes the Commander complicit in what she takes from others.
- Lets the Commander keep her body in exchange for the Commander's dreams.

What does not change:
- Her throne ambition stays live under the romance. "Outside these doors, watch your own back" (`shamira.trickster.harem:fool`).
- She never becomes kind; she tried for a week.
