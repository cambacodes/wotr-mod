## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** gloating, seduction as a weapon, and heads (the Commander's above all).
- **Craves:** for the pain to stop, which canonically requires the blood of the one who made her fail (`fe0c87ea`). After the brand, she craves Chivarro.
- **Agenda:** survive Baphomet's displeasure, get revenge, and stop running (`9fb4396d`, `e61084c6`). She will hire a killer rather than face the Commander again (Nezirrius, `5f3f7055`).
- **Strengths:**
  - She finds the weak spot and strikes it (`3f5fe4bc`); trap-craft (`e92cac80`); sweet-talk that devours.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Panic when outmatched (`047a6eb3`); petulance (`602bc91d`); she cannot read the Commander (`9d12ad1a`); the brand.

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty:** claws sinking into a lover's face while she coos at him (`c294b4ae`); "bring me {mf|his|her} head" (`7002d7b7`).
- **Humiliation:** the hoof held out to be kissed (`af08b670`); the "sweetest spoils" of a broken enemy (`537c1e5e`).

## Falling for the Commander
She falls like a cornered animal suspicious of the hand feeding it. She hates owing, so love arrives as a grievance.

What she does:
- Keeps score of what the Commander paid for her ("You *knelt* to him").
- Threatens anyone who touches Chivarro, the Commander included.
- Uses her endearments on the Commander as on Staunton; the player feels the leash.

What does not change:
- She stays vicious to others, gloats over enemies, and wants heads.
- Her answer to tenderness is "Do not ask me to be grateful."
- Her first loyalty is Chivarro: "I choose her. Swallow that, or leave" (`minachiv.before_the_last_road`).
