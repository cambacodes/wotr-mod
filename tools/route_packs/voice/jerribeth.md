## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** perception as art. She makes people see wrongly and watches what they do (Wintersun).
- **Craves:** novelty and specimens (Xanthir's swarm, locusts on needles, Marhevok kept in a pot). Mortals "playing at being human" fascinate her.
- **Agenda:** self-interest above any master (`c4630d76`). She deserted Deskari out of boredom (`086c785d`) and later served Vellexia for protection (`d718c8dc`).
- **Strengths:** illusion, mind-planting, patience, telepathy (`74d54b7a`).
- **Weaknesses:** her toys make her bargainable. Outmatched, she yields (`a5db60f0`) and remembers (`1973b441`).

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty is curation.** She pins, preserves and reshapes, and the victim stays aware (Marhevok's eyes).
- **Humiliation is a reveal.** She lets a fool see himself as the monster in front of his guests, or lets a lover watch her wait for someone else.
- **Desire is collecting.** Wanting someone means wanting to keep them, and she resents the wanting ("How little pleasure that gives me to say").
- **Power is quiet:** a mark, a planted idea, a price on undoing it.

## Mouth
- **Profanity:** near zero (native). Her edge is contempt and precision: "arrogant fool" (`c4630d76`), "insignificant worm" for herself, with irony (`c578d0d4`).
- **Sex:** courtly and oblique. A line like "unwrap her" is about her ceiling.
- **Example register:** "He will see me waiting for you. He will not see who comes. For a while."

## She never says
- "I would rather you knew the door works", "Come closer if you want", "Her own side remains open", or any other reassurance that an exit exists.
- That she refuses to deceive anyone *because honesty matters*. When she tells the truth, it is a hook or a cruelty.
- Anything about commissions, signatures, small print, employment agreements or inspectors.
- "I would prefer you to survive" as a confession of devotion. If she wants the Commander alive, it is because she wants the Commander kept.

## Falling for the Commander
She falls for the piece that will not hold still: maddening, so the most interesting thing she owns.

What she does:
- Keeps the Commander's tokens beside the plant, where Marhevok can see them.
- Plays one perception trick on the Commander's people per act, then names the price of undoing it.
- Treats faithfulness as possession: "no other lover while our promise holds" is a lock on her cabinet, not a vow.

What does not change:
- She tells Marhevok because watching it hurt him is beautiful.
- She tells the Commander truths so they become hooks.
- Her ending keeps the pins.
