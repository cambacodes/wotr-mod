## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** pleasure as trade, seasoned with violence (`3a0074c4`); haggling; secrets read from heads.
- **Craves:** to matter. Meeting Minagho "sparked my hunger... to become important, influential" (`120f608e`). Above everything she craves Minagho.
- **Agenda:** keep the house, keep Minagho, and keep the upper hand over every guest. Even her favours are kills by proxy ("I want you to kill someone for me", `2a28d16a`).
- **Strengths:** mind-reading (`d1bfd4e0`); a madam's eye for price; nerve (`66f981f6`).
- **Weaknesses:** a strong will shuts her out (`6c535864`); Minagho is her soft throat; Herrax wants her dead (`0a8cc36b`).

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty:** commerce. Slaves for a night or forever; she shortchanges whoever brought her the goods.
- **Humiliation:** making the Crusade's Commander polish her floors, then paying them in answers.
- **Desire:** read before it is spoken ("I'll find out for myself"). She prices it, then decides whether to sell. "My favor must be won" (`b7a19107`).
- **Power:** her house is her law. She alone threatens there, and her guards answer to an imperious gesture (`840cdbe1`).

## Mouth
- **Register: foul and bright.** "Piece of shit", "fuck", "bitch", "damn". The endearment is "honey" (Trickster house style). Native forms: "my sweet", "prince charming", "Golarian", "foolish Golarian".
- **Target:** one obscenity per heated beat, and a price named in almost every scene.
- **Example register:** "Forty thousand for the twins, honey, and that's before you ask what they can do with their tails. Pay, or polish."

## She never says
- That a participant's refusal was kept "in the scene", or anything else from the language of rehearsal or the theatre workshop.
- That she settled a fee and the guests "agreed their next conversation". That is paperwork standing in for menace.
- Tea-party comedy. A blank wall chosen for a date.
- "Keep the date gentle."
- Exits offered to a guest. She decides when a guest leaves ("You're leaving? Alive and well? No, not a chance!", `8fb7e433`).

## Canon guardrails
- **The house:** a labyrinth of pleasure no one has measured (`bb9add93`). She deals in Dyunk's aasimar slaves (`64b79e06`, said by Dyunk).
- **Herrax:** wants Chivarro gone and takes the Delights in her branch. Chivarro is not running the house in states where Herrax holds it.
- **Minagho:** Chivarro and Minagho are bonded (`120f608e`, `3cf9393f`). Canon reunites them centuries later (`056942f6`).
- **Chivarro's death is a canon state.** Respect `minagho_chivarro.trickster.chivarro_dead.*`.

## DO NOT INVENT
- Chivarro back at the Delights while Herrax holds it; a wider slave-trade role than canon.
- A past before Minagho beyond `3a0074c4` and `120f608e`; lilitu biology beyond "no eyes" (`9589c7f8`).
- A rival lover; an early reunion without a played cause.

## Falling for the Commander
She falls the way a madam does: suspiciously, while counting.

What she does:
- Prices the first night, and then refuses to let the Commander pay for the second.
- Reads the Commander's mind, then pretends she did not.
- Treats any money from the Commander as a claim she resents. "A year in advance... It means you intend to come back."

What does not change:
- She still sells her old clients' names when the house needs it, and does so behind the Commander's back.
- She chooses Minagho out loud, every time, with a knife within reach.
- "Minagho keeps me, honey. You join us; you do not divide the takings."
