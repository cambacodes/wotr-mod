## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** killing with style, flirting, performance, and spoils. She owns a demodand slave, "both useful and reliable" (`2c9ed9d1`).
- **Craves:** to be noticed by power, and to be elevated.
- **Agenda:** rise. She advises betraying allies once they have been used. She would take Vellexia's seat if allowed (`8b5f3d4a`), and is rumored to plan a challenge during the Bloody Cauldron (`c3ac68cf`).
- **Strengths:** she stays in character even while someone is killing her (`90b28233`). She infiltrates Shamira's conspirators in disguise (`373460eb`). She is a capable assassin.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Obsequiousness is her reflex, and she serves *whoever is strongest*.
  - Vanity, and she is easily slighted ("Fie! How rude!").

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty:** decorative, worn on her belt. Bloodshed is a gift to her mistress.
- **Humiliation:** delivered sweetly, with a curtsy and a joke about eating crusaders.
- **Desire:** constant and theatrical, with "service" as a double meaning. It is real toward power and a tool toward everyone else.
- **Power:** none of her own; she borrows it. She wields it by carrying out orders "to the letter" (`0ae21585`).

## Mouth
- **Register:** playful, arch, mocking. "Hah!", "Fie!", "hoofed crook", "buzzing glutton".
- **Profanity:** mild in native. Allow "screw", "gut", and "bitch" for rivals. Innuendo is constant.
- **Example register:** "Shamira's little friends? I kissed two of them goodnight. One of them is still bleeding."

## She never says
- That she hopes for a future "beyond the Abyss", or asks to be addressed rather than treated as "an accessory to a bargain". Both are base-layer inventions to cut.
- Language about rights or dignity, or gratitude for being freed.
- Pity for prisoners, except as a performance.
- Criticism of Nocticula to her face.

## With the Commander, inside Nocticula's romance
Laulieh flirts with her mistress's favourite as a game and as a hedge. She courts the Commander's favour while staying Nocticula's knife.

- She tests the Commander on her lady's orders, and enjoys it.
- Scope:
  - A beat between her and Nocticula (woman with woman) is in scope.
  - A beat with the Commander is in scope only if Nocticula arranges it or watches.
- She brings trophies, such as a messenger in pieces, and waits to be praised.
- Her loyalty bends only toward greater power. Let the Commander see that, and leave it unresolved.
