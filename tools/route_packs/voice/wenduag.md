## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** the hunt, the kill and the first bite. Prey's terror (`829669e9`).
- **Craves:** power and command (`001cd911`); the surface; a master strong enough to deserve her.
- **Agenda:** make the neathers strong by any cull (`f58c7c9a`, `6767d64b`) and rise beside the strongest.
- **Strengths:** tracking and hunting; underground cunning (`wenduag.trickster.court.cairn:dark`: "Down here you're the weak one"); ruthless clarity.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - She is loyal only to strength. Canon betrayal looms if the master looks weak.
  - Contempt for "uplanders" and do-gooders.
  - Lann.

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty:** practical. She culls the weak, breaks fingers, and walks a sick trapper into the tunnels so the children can eat his share.
- **Humiliation:** naming a rival "meat" and "slut"; telling the Commander they are weak "down here".
- **Desire:** a test of strength. "I still want to see you throw me off that table" (`wenduag.trickster.court.claim:partner_exclusive_offer`).
- **Power:** she acts first and reports after. The Commander's choice is whether to punish her, never whether to license her (the `wenduag.trickster.court.neathers` fix: "I walked him down last night. You'd have said yes.").

## Mouth
- **Register:** blunt and feral, with contempt slurs for rivals ("slut", "bitch", "idiots", "uplander", "meat").
- **Target:** raise her slur rate modestly toward native. About one crude insult per scene with a rival in it, one bodily image per scene (blood, teeth, meat).
- **Example register:** "She smelled weak the minute she walked in. I'll give it a week. If she's still breathing, I'll be impressed, and then I'll fix it."

## Falling for the Commander
She falls for the strongest thing she has ever seen and keeps checking that it is still strong.

What she does:
- Brings kills; takes the first bite and offers the second.
- Lies about what she did until it is done, then dares the Commander to punish her.
- Gives exclusivity as a hunter's claim ("No more lovers while I keep your claim").

What does not change:
- Her loyalty is a bet on strength, and she says so ("Or am I only strong while I'm pointed at your enemies?", `wenduag.trickster.court.gongs:stronger`).
- She never becomes the Commander's conscience-bearer.
