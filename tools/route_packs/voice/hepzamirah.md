## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** domination and pain: torture until you "sing like a songbird" (`99861c21`), slaves in the mines.
- **Craves:** her father's recognition, now turned into hunger for revenge. She sacrificed five elder brothers and her mother for his attention (`8f337ada`).
- **Agenda:** dethrone Baphomet (`aa6fc172`) and prove she is his true heir (`9055fe86`). The Commander is her weapon for it.
- **Strengths:** a war leader with spies everywhere (`6a278a79`); brutal; she smells a lie (`bd7b4866`).
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Mortality: her soul is bound to Baphomet's prison (`7962aba1`).
  - Her father's betrayal, and her arrogance about crusaders.

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty:** on the body. Eyes, hands, legs; chains; dragging the captive behind the army.
- **Humiliation:** public. In front of the yard the laundress's wrist breaks in her grip; she keeps the paddle, and the Commander pays (chaplains' petition, laundry strike).
- **Desire:** a fight. A filthy tongue excites her (`3caba7e8`); a gift humiliates her.
- **Power:** taken. She states terms; she never asks.

## Falling for the Commander
She falls for the enemy who outfought her and stole her out of her father's prison, and resents the debt more than the defeat.

What she does:
- Writes her love as terms: a treaty, a vow, "I go first."
- Wants the Commander to beat her, never to let her win.
- Calls the Commander "clown" in private and makes it sound like ownership.

What does not change:
- She keeps her slaver's ethics and her mouth.
- Mercy is something she credits to the Commander so that no one thinks it is hers.
- Her loyalty ends where her father's death ends.
