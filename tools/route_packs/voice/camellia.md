## Appetite, cravings, agenda
- **Appetite:** killing, specifically the moment of understanding in the victim's face. Blood for the spirits.
- **Craves:** relief that does not come (`81d93c2e`); excitement ("anyone who seeks dangerous and exciting adventures should travel with you", `0b4d9b0c`); secrets.
- **Agenda:** feed Mireya, keep her mask, and keep her friend close enough to kill.
- **Strengths:** a flawless mask; shamanic sense (`00373a9c`); ruthless practicality.
- **Weaknesses:** boredom (`4dd21f1d`); the urge she does not control; contempt for commoners; her mother's story (`744a8ca4`).

## Cruelty, humiliation, desire, power
- **Cruelty:** patient and private. Names go on a list; the knife comes later. She enjoys the waiting.
- **Humiliation:** a perfect lady's put-down, delivered with a smile ("commoner"; the beard remarks, `3c458399`).
- **Desire:** inseparable from wanting to kill. "I do not want to feel the desire to kill you" (`4819461c`) is her love line.
- **Power:** being underestimated. The amiable mask is the weapon.

## Mouth
- **Profanity: zero (prim)** in her normal register.
- **Exception:** native lets one cold "bitch" or "damn" through at the extremity of the mother reveal (`e3fbe2de`). Use at most one such crack per route, and only at a matching extremity.
- **Her edge:** perfect politeness laid over obscene intent, and lip-licking at blood.
- **Example register:** "Do sit down, Sergeant. You look so tired. I always think the tired ones go quietest."

## She never says

- Anything that sounds like being cured, or "had not been bored for some time".
- A promise not to kill the Commander. The canon stance is that she never said she wouldn't (`camellia.trickster.epilogue.kept`).
- Crude sex talk. Her sensuality is all lips, blood and attention.

## Canon guardrails
- **Mireya** is canon (`81d93c2e`). Her teacher was a stern shaman (`362ef53b`).
- **Her family:** her father is Horgus Gwerm (`6a6c6f54`). He killed her mother Iris when he caught Iris trying to smother Camellia (`e301dd6b`, `3cc889a5`, `744a8ca4`).
- **Her record:** she killed five people under his roof (`720360c5`).
- **Her endings** never let her stay and stop:
  - she vanishes, bored (`4dd21f1d`);
  - she makes "a futile attempt to drive a dagger into the former Commander's heart" (`bde81fb1`);
  - she returns and leaves again: "I do not want to feel the desire to kill you" (`4819461c`).
- **The most her story earns** is staying with the desire intact. The epilogue keeps someone dead: a name crossed off the list after the war, never the Commander's.

## Falling for the Commander
She falls for the Commander as the one exception she has not yet decided about: "You're wondering whether I'll do it. So am I." The knife on the pillow, point toward the door, is the right shape.

What she does:
- Spares the Commander and kills elsewhere, and lets the Commander know or suspect.
- Shows love as attention: she watches the Commander's face as she watches a victim's.
- Names the revocability of her choice herself, as a delight. She never speaks of it as an apology.
