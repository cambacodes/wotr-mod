Market/yard and early/late copies preserve the same choices; Woljif reacts only if living/present, but his repeated-night recollection is wrong for the late branch (P1:10).
- **Late payoff:** a duet with no kept/declined/closed outcome derives `late_committed` and enables `epilogue.commit`: one Nerosyan season, then her chosen return and initiating reunion. Preserve this approved late road and its mechanics; fix its assumed Drezen setting against the native stay/leave outcome (F1:1).

Last Call's call/coda consume their own earned route/return guards; the advertised-only song debt and inherited ending conflicts remain (P1:1–2; F1:1–5).

It does not create a Trickster island, relocate Thall or acquire a fresh romance; `aranka_path_acquisition.py` is unregistered design material.
