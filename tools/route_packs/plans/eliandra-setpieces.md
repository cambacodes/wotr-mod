## Character, reservation and binding limits

Eliandra is Pulura's Chaotic Good aasimar high priestess: exacting about observation, protective of her people, capable of tenderness and a severe refusal. She gave up marriage and family, received extraordinary strength, and banned romances when the lovers' quarrels disrupted collective research. Release lets her want a lover without abandoning either faith or survivors. Her sexual inexperience is an authored interpretation, not a native anatomical or biographical fact; never claim canon establishes virginity. Desire should be blunt once she recognizes it, with brief imperious directions and awkward practical corrections, not endless maidenly bewilderment. Her decision to serve at thirteen is history only; every intimate situation concerns the present-day woman after a century of service.

**Exact registered reservation:** `bargain`; Chapter 5; `Pulura star-heart offering basin, then Drezen north-gate milestone and emptied dry-fall chart table`; payoff `irreversible lights or priestly strength are relinquished without buying love; her road proposal keeps the survivors, and charts meet Katair’s morning objection`. Keep the divine counteroffer counted as a bargain, the eighth in the atlas. Her pursuit supplies agency within that reservation, not a new device. No false-proof/forged-record device, aphrodisiac, drunken commitment or new rescue thread.

Avoid the atlas bargain cluster's price → test → room → reward procession: the rite grants release; her road proposal grants commitment; she chooses the emptied workroom for intimacy; her people remain the relationship's active consequence. Avoid portable tokens as universal invitations, every woman's ledger-as-courtship, generic grief consolation and interchangeable private rooms. `atlas-0018` specifically duplicates Irabeth's “She takes it like a blow she saw coming”: both first-mile `no` variants instead have Eliandra look north, assess the road her people must take, and answer without that stock image. Do not borrow Kiana's mid-act husband interruption. Katair's arrival remains the **morning after**, motivated by his return to the evacuated sanctuary.

Binding (1): deliberate captive attack is an irreversible closure, not a rescue opportunity. (2): matrix/user closures stand. (3): letters are not bodily presence; actual departure and earned return must carry into all consumers; no living Commander scenes after unreturned sacrifice. (4): the authored release and any dependent native alterations require current Trickster power; other paths retain native events. (5): preserve legitimate existing access, checks and lock-ins; do not force yes for skipped, failed, refused or closed histories. No Shyka prerequisite or echo: Eliandra has no allocated echo, and the page never supplies desire, confession or release. No visions, another-life recollections, maxims or editorial commentary in proposed player prose.
