# Proposed EDGE row (coordinator-owned rubric integration)

Source: edge-audit.md §12 and CHARACTER-TRUTH. This is an authored tooling proposal, not player text or a canon claim. Pass remains **every dimension ≥91**. Lexical findings are advisory evidence, never automatic proof of characterization or a numeric prose score.

| Code | Dimension | 91+ means | Automatic caps |
|---|---|---|---|
| EDGE | Canon appetite, menace and native register | Every woman retains her native goals, appetites, agency and mouth. For unrepentant villains, every chapter-band has an on-page or confirmed cruel act or credible threat with cost or complicity for the Commander. No ethical principles ventriloquized through demons; a beneficial act has her selfish motive on the page. At most one victim-confrontation scene per unrepentant route, with no narration celebrating justice served. No announced freedom to leave, stop or refuse, nor body positioning to provide an exit; canon danger (e.g. Arueshalae's draining touch) may be stated once as danger. Documents are devices: about at most one in four scenes resolves through paperwork/negotiation; business vocabulary does not exceed menace vocabulary. Profanity, slurs, contempt and sexual frankness match her native register (prim can be zero), checked with three native and three authored lines. At pivotal beats she acts first or demands at least as often as she asks permission; the Commander is not her moral authority. At most one unguarded vulnerability beat per act, with distinct tenderness across women; endings preserve appetite or threat where canon requires it. Scenes play in the world with choices, native pressures and reactions. | Any §12 hard-rule failure (empty menace band, moral ventriloquism, penance tour, announced exits beyond the canon-danger exception): EDGE ≤60. Villain redeemed without a native basis, therapy/HR choreography, paperwork replacing villainy or moral deference replacing agency: EDGE ≤60. Noticeably cleaner than native, generic repeated tenderness, or an ending curing an unrepentant villain: EDGE ≤80. A claim of canon-justified softening requires cited enGB evidence. |

The proposed caps translate §12's hard-fail rule into the existing 0–100 table; the coordinator owns approval of those numeric caps. Do not apply villain menace requirements to virtuous women or demand swearing from a prim character. Arueshalae's redeemed/fallen histories must be judged separately. Softening toward loved ones is allowed while evil values, methods and appetites remain.

All existing Binding contexts (1)–(5) remain authoritative: player/user closures stand, presence and return must be earned, canon changes are Trickster-only, and earned gates legitimately exclude outcomes. EDGE never requires new mechanics, gates, costs or access everywhere. Shyka's paid page gates but does not earn an outcome; the foresight/echo discipline and registry allocations still bind. DLC-tier additions and explained native rewrites are allowed, labelled authored, save-compatible and Trickster-only; remove contradictions only to the extent the event explains. No fourth-wall or vision justification.

Use `python tools/edge_lint.py --story <fresh-export> --json <outside-repo-report>` for quoted scene/location evidence, per-layer lexical rates, business:menace screen, own-speaker profanity and cross-route stock tenderness. Counts include every exported alternative, clone and retired scene, not a selected playthrough. Bare words such as `rights`, `report`, `the door` and `treatment` require context review; motives, real acts, penance framing and native register require human reading and game-data verification. The tool does not award a ≥91 score.

`--strict` freezes displayed scene text only for `tools/route_packs/voice_locks.json` IDs (a JSON list or `{"scenes": ["scene.id"]}`). Gate/flag changes do not fail. No lock file means no locked scenes yet. The before-baseline is not a lock list. After approved rewrites, the coordinator can store the scene's new `text_hash(scene)` in the lock object's optional `scene_text_sha256` map; this preserves the original before-numbers. Without an override the baseline hash applies. `--write-baseline --revision <revision>` is an explicit capture operation; strict cannot refresh its own baseline. Unknown or removed locked scenes fail. The tool never edits story text, gates or the shared rubric.
