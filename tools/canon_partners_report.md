Every incoming scene/node/choice context is considered by the lint; names are discovery signals, not evidence that the arrangement is adequate or the partner has consented.

Manual classification follows the user’s rule: a living current partner must know and react; the default is coexistence. A native breakup already dramatized with a reaction is not silently rewritten into an ongoing relationship. Unrequited interest, coercion, disguise claims and DLC anomalies are expressly qualified. GAP means acknowledgement/continuity is missing; it does not itself prove cheating. No unambiguous new CONTRADICTION is established in this sweep. Choosing a shorter route, declining a return, or keeping an evil woman evil is not a defect by itself.

No new knowledge is granted by foresight or echoes. Historical dead flags/latches are not a current body; returns require their existing earned return and body/availability contracts. Memory-only partners must not be staged as living actors. Native Aeon outcomes, Lich reanimation, and DLC anomalous counterparts are separate histories. Suggested repairs below are directions for an authorized writer, not implemented prose or proposed new requirements.
