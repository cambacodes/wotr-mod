# Expanded roster and delivery plan

This document records the user's revised scope and supersedes the earlier requirement that every romance work on every mythic path.
The roster is a delivery and research inventory, not a claim that the listed routes have been implemented.
The user confirmed the side-conversation is lost and requested an independent script-based rebuild with at least 25 distinct characters.
This roster uses local game text, blueprint references and installed-mod evidence rather than treating the lost conversation as a dependency.

## Path and content requirements

Every eligible adult romance character needs a bespoke, attainable Trickster route.
Ember and Aivu are official full-content friendship and companion routes, with equally substantial arcs, choices, art, introductions, endings and verification.
Their themes differ from the adult romances; their production priority and quality bar do not.
Trickster access for both must be designed around their actual quests and companion systems.
Other mythic paths retain character-appropriate restrictions and reactions.
Changing paths must have authored consequences and tested state transitions.
A Trickster intervention must create a real opportunity through quest connections, restored contact or credible fate changes; it does not manufacture affection or silently complete unrelated quests.

Existing marriages and partnerships do not automatically exclude a candidate.
Affairs, separation, negotiated arrangements and reconciliation need distinct consequences where used.
Optional Commander-plus-two-women relationships require independent mutual attraction between the women as well as their relationships with the Commander.
Declining a group relationship must not silently erase an otherwise viable individual relationship.
Coercive magic, compelled undead service and patron authority are not evidence of voluntary affection.
Adult romance uses mature themes and non-graphic intimacy.
Ember and Aivu receive no adult-romance or sexual content, and no age-changing workaround is planned.

## Required route specification

The user's minimum is a hard release requirement: every resulting character arc must match or exceed the amount and quality of a fully fledged RanRomance character route.
This applies equally to friendship, experimental, existing-route extensions and new romances.
The installed readme advertises approximately 16,000 to 19,000-plus words for most full routes, with a shorter Nocticula chain.
Do not select that shortest chain as a loophole for the general per-character requirement.
Audit actual player-facing RanRomance content and use the largest fully developed comparable route as the conservative benchmark.
Until that audit is complete, plan at least 21,000 meaningful words per resulting character arc, increasing the floor if the measured benchmark requires it.
This is a planning floor, not a substitute for the final comparison.
The user requires the Anevia-Irabeth-Commander triad to be at least twice as long as a fully developed single-character route because it develops two women.
Its combined meaningful-content floor is therefore 42,000 words under the current 21,000-word single-character benchmark, and rises to twice any revised higher benchmark.
This combined floor does not replace the independent development requirements for Anevia and Irabeth.
The arc must develop each woman's relationship with the Commander, their continuing marriage, and the three-person relationship, including consequences and endings for the relevant choices.
Count shared text once toward the combined 42,000-word total; repeating it in two character inventories cannot satisfy the requirement.

The [installed-source inventory](reference/art-review/ran-route-word-inventory.json) traces localization references through each actual route initializer and its callbacks.
Using one consistent tokenizer, the configured keyed-word counts are Nocticula 6,265, Nurah 14,715, Targona 19,004, Terendelev 15,199, Aranka 16,556 and Minagho 16,324.
Targona remains the largest identified route, with 18,359 words after exact normalized-text deduplication.
These are static configured-text counts, not single-playthrough lengths or quality scores.
The audit still records helper/revision attribution limits and loader warnings; the provisional floor is not a claim that every supporting word has already been allocated.
The 69 unallocated configured helper keys contain another 1,138 words.
Assigning all of them to the largest route would produce a conservative upper bound of 20,142 configured words.
Seven additional unmapped localization keys contain 218 words, making the deliberately overinclusive source-inventory envelope 20,360 words.
The planning floor has therefore been raised from the earlier 20,000 to 21,000 meaningful words per resulting character arc while exact helper ownership is resolved.
This deliberately avoids using an incomplete route-only count to set the minimum too low.
Use `python tools/measure-story-content.py development/Story.json` to regenerate the development inventory with the same tokenizer.
That inventory separates prose, choices and exact repeated segments, and leaves external-content credit and attainable-playthrough totals unassigned until supported by evidence.

Count verified integrated native or RanRomance material toward the resulting arc where appropriate rather than rewriting it merely to inflate addon size.
Report original material and newly authored material separately.
Exclude repeated localization, copied passages, boilerplate, unreachable branches, documentation and interchangeable filler from the content comparison.
Track both total meaningful branch content and the substantial arc a player can actually experience in one coherent playthrough.
Shared scenes count toward a character's depth only when that character has material development in them; do not credit an entire group script equally to every cameo.
Scene count, word count and review scores are separate evidence and none can establish completion alone.

Required quality includes sustained characterization, varied settings and activities, earned intimacy or friendship, quest consequences, ordinary shared life, difficult decisions, optional content and developed endings.
Aim for writing that fits the original game's voice and presentation without claiming indistinguishability before review and playtesting.
The currently delivered short drafts are foundations and do not satisfy this full-route content floor.

The [per-character readiness audit](development/per-character-readiness.md) records the existing content shortfalls and missing full-route evidence.
Earlier passage-level scores cannot be promoted into full-route approval.
Apply the release requirement to each character individually; a large roster or an exceptional route cannot compensate for another character's incomplete arc.
For each character, the completion record must link the measured content, a coherent playable route from entry to ending, the canon comparison, reviewed artwork and integration results.
Review the complete assembled experience, including pacing and transitions between existing and new scenes, before calling a route fully fledged.
