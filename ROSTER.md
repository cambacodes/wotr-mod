This document records the user's revised scope: Trickster must have a bespoke attainable route to every eligible romance character, while other mythic paths retain character-appropriate restrictions, motives and consequences.
The roster is a delivery and research inventory, not a claim that the listed routes have been implemented.
The user's intended installation includes every DLC, and routes may use DLC dialogue, characters, events and supported assets where they fit.
Detect content availability separately from whether the current save has visited or completed its events.

## Path and content requirements

Character fidelity and consequential gameplay are hard acceptance constraints for every listed character and every route, including existing work, alternate branches, group relationships, recovery and endings.
The expansion must preserve recognizable motives, intelligence, agency, conflicts and moral differences; a character cannot become agreeable merely to permit romance or solve a scene.
Changes in trust and desire must follow earned situations and sustained development.
Good and evil Commander choices must have distinct, character-consistent reactions and consequences where appropriate.
When a scene promises a quest, resource, access, survival or ending consequence, the implementation and verification must establish that consequence rather than relying on dialogue alone.
Reviewers must reject failures of these constraints regardless of word count or an average review score.
Measure both shorter and longer compatible completed relationship paths; a long maximum cannot excuse an underlength committed route.
Refusing physical intimacy must not be the reason a completed romance lacks the required substantive relationship content.
Early refusals and deliberately abandoned routes are separate outcomes, not completed romances to pad to the minimum.
Apply the same gate retrospectively to already written routes; prior scoped approval does not substitute for assembled-route review.

Every listed adult romance character needs a bespoke, attainable Trickster route, including credible authored recovery after missed quests, deaths or hostile outcomes.
Trickster must support concurrent successful routes with ToyBox Free Love and No Jealousy enabled, through prepared quest connections and earned interventions rather than automatic acquiescence.
Availability across paths does not mean identical scenes or frictionless consent; each path must offer a believable opportunity while preserving the character's agency, values, boundaries and consequences.
Other mythic paths may retain character-appropriate restrictions or unavailable outcomes, as the user explicitly allowed in the revised path requirement.
Existing all-path drafts remain available for review, but their existence does not justify erasing a character's motives to force access.
The route matrix must account for Angel, Aeon, Azata, Demon, Devil, Gold Dragon, Legend, Lich, Swarm-that-Walks and Trickster, including legitimate path changes and irreversible transitions.
Ember and Aivu are official full-content friendship and companion candidates, with equally substantial arcs, choices, art, introductions, endings and verification; their access still requires a credible account of native campaign history.
Their themes differ from the adult romances, but their eventual quality and content requirements remain equal.
The user's latest priority is to defer further Ember and Aivu work until the adult romance partners are complete and the first in-game test has taken place.
Preserve their existing manuscripts, integration and reviews while prioritizing adult romance delivery.
Access for Ember and Aivu must be designed around their actual quests and companion systems on every mythic path, including paths that threaten or remove their native companion status.
Every path needs a character-appropriate introduction and credible way to continue, even when it changes through the Commander’s allies, quest choices, restored contact, political leverage or fate intervention.
Changing paths must have authored consequences and tested state transitions without silently deleting an established relationship.
A Trickster intervention must create a real opportunity through quest connections, restored contact or credible fate changes; it does not manufacture affection or silently complete unrelated quests.

Existing marriages and partnerships do not automatically exclude a candidate.
Affairs, separation, negotiated arrangements and reconciliation need distinct consequences where used.
Optional Commander-plus-two-women relationships require independent mutual attraction between the women as well as their relationships with the Commander.
Declining a group relationship must not silently erase an otherwise viable individual relationship.
Coercive magic, compelled undead service and patron authority are not evidence of voluntary affection.
Adult romance uses mature themes and graphic and explicit intimacy.
Ember and Aivu receive no adult-romance or sexual content, and no age-changing workaround is planned.
