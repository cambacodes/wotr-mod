# Current character art direction

The user's latest clarification permits imperfections and unusual features when they improve resemblance to the original design.
Adult romance characters should be attractive, humanized and recognizably adult without becoming generic idealized humans.
There is no absolute ban on wrinkles, asymmetry, scars or other distinctive imperfections.
Preserve imperfections that improve likeness to the original character; removing them is not inherently an improvement.
Keep the overall presentation appealing rather than heavily aged or unattractive; do not manufacture a visibly elderly appearance merely to satisfy an earlier review criterion.
Apply this consistently to demons, anthropomorphic animal characters, dwarves, gnomes, dragons in humanoid forms and other eligible adult races.
Favor appealing human facial proportions, expressive eyes and an approachable mouth while retaining distinctive racial and character traits.
Do not make petite adult races look like children.

Recognition should come from a deliberate combination of the original palette, eyes, markings, hairstyle or crest, ears, horns, wings, fur, scales, stature, costume motifs and expression.
Choose the strongest identity cues for each character rather than reproducing every monstrous anatomical feature.
Avoid both an unchanged creature face and a generic human design that loses the character's identity.
These are intentional attractive redesigns grounded in native references, not claims of pixel-exact native likeness.

Visual rejuvenation does not rewrite canonical chronology, relationships or personality.
For Soana, preserve her adult dwarf identity, silver hair, blue markings, practical forest setting and established history while avoiding the heavily aged treatment previously requested.
Subtle imperfections that strengthen her likeness are welcome; flawless skin is not a requirement.
The earlier reviewer requirement for visibly elderly facial anatomy no longer governs her visual acceptance.
The user subsequently rejected Soana-v10 specifically for its old, jagged and wrinkled face.
Soana revisions must visibly soften severe facial contours and prominent age lines while preserving her silver hair, brown eyes, blue markings and sturdy adult dwarf proportions.
Review the delivered face for conventional attractiveness; retention of character traits does not excuse repeating the rejected aged treatment.
For Jerribeth, preserve her recognizable demonic palette, crest and wing or appendage cues, but humanize the face and mouth instead of making the native insectile face or long proboscis mandatory.
The recently generated native-form Jerribeth-v2 is therefore a reference iteration, not the final target under this revised brief.

The user finds Chivarro-v2 beautiful but less appealing than Minagho and requests further facial refinement.
Her next revision should aim for comparable appeal through softer cheek and brow transitions, a more expressive mouth and less rigid facial shading, while retaining her own face, warm blond hair, horns, eyeless lilitu traits and composed bearing.
Do not make her a copy of Minagho or treat prior passing scores as grounds to disregard this preference.
The existing v2 warm-dress assignment remains a reviewed candidate until an actual replacement passes independent review.

Ember and Aivu remain non-romantic friendship characters with their established ages and age-appropriate presentation.
This art instruction does not change their route scope or authorize adult-romance redesigns of them.

## Review criteria

Review actual delivered revisions for attractiveness under this brief, recognizable identity, coherent racial traits, clearly adult presentation where applicable, anatomy, composition, painterly game consistency and story fit.
Balance attractiveness with recognizable likeness, allowing identity-defining imperfections instead of automatically polishing them away.
Do not penalize intentional humanization solely because the native model differs, or require removal of every wrinkle or unusual feature.
Do flag loss of identifying features, generic faces, childish presentation of adult characters, broken anatomy and inconsistent racial cues.
Earlier scores remain historical assessments under their recorded briefs and must not be represented as acceptance under this new one.
The above-90 target still requires independent review of the actual revision, with no guaranteed score.
Runtime crops and in-game presentation remain separate verification requirements.
