### Status and priority keys

- **E** means an exact existing local manuscript scene/page, whether registered or draft; it still needs assignment review.
- **P-W** means a Writer reviewer proposal, not proof that the event exists in this checkout.
- **P-A** means an art exploration proposed here, with no claim of an implemented scene.
- **P0** means currently being explored by an assigned worker; do not duplicate its output files.
- **P1** means the next breadth batch, favoring existing references and characters lacking event art.
- **P2** means subsequent breadth or a native-reference intake before generation.
- **D** means deferred friendship art, not a romance backlog.

Paths abbreviated `C/`, `O/`, `S/`, and `R/` mean `art/candidates/`, `art/expansion/originals/`, `art/CustomNpcPortraits/RanRomance-Tirabade/Scenes/`, and `reference/art-review/` respectively.

Named native directories contain provenance records as well as images; the generator must inspect the actual reference image before using it.
An extracted file named FullLengthImage can still be only a small portrait crop and must not be treated as proof of an unseen body or costume.

## Generation and selection rules during the pause

Preserve attractive adult humanization, original identifying traits, native costume exposure and body silhouette where supported.
An authored private garment is allowed and must differ through cut and construction, not only color.
Do not default to covering a demon or putting a civilian in combat gear.
Retain mature racial identity and character-specific demon or succubus sensuality rather than reducing every character to a modest human face with accessory horns.
Balance humanization with identifying fur, ears, skin, horns and other supported traits; race alone does not establish temperament or current arousal.
Do not infer unseen full-body proportions from a head thumbnail.
Reference gaps should be stated, with the result labeled an interpretation.

Each useful alternate should change a real pose, action, camera direction or emotional state.
Do not produce two portraits with the same head lean and smile and call the second a date scene.
Use sensible fabric weight, weave, layered opacity, gravity folds, joint compression and contact with chairs or props.
Check period-appropriate tailoring, seams, closures and layering against the character's resources, the garment's construction and the actual pose.
Do not present kitsune or dragon heat cycles as established game lore without source evidence.
Any proposed physiology of that kind must be labeled authored alternate development and tied to a compatible adult scene, not inferred from race.
Keep the customizable Commander out of frame unless deliberately producing an explicitly labeled player-specific variant.
Adult sensuality is appropriate; mature/graphic and explicit scene art need not be emotionally neutral.
Ember and Aivu remain outside all adult-romance batches.

Candidate records should retain character, version, input-reference paths, intended E/P-W/P-A scope and actual output path.
For generated concepts without an existing page, record `assignment: none`.
Do not stage, change page fields, claim a passing score or advertise an in-game-ready scene merely because an image was generated.
Existing automatic page switching is sufficient for the identified events; no selector or new UI work is part of this art-only queue.

The next review should judge whole sequences as well as individual files, including distinct expressions, native identity, attractive anatomy, costume, fabric and precise selected-event fit.
All eventual approval remains revision-specific and independent.
