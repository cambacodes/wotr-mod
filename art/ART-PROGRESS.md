## Full art-run completion gate

The user explicitly directed that art production continue until every character has a complete, reviewed art set, including intimate or explicit route moments and endings.
Do not stop after portraits or first-pass candidates, and do not report completion from file counts.
For every route, audit the actual available pages and branches, identify distinct important visual beats, and record a scene-matched assignment decision for each beat.
The set must cover the character's identity and signature design, meaningful courtship or conflict scenes, adult intimate/explicit milestones where the route contains them, and every materially distinct ending outcome.
If a branch has no eligible scene art, record why and verify that its ending or intimate state is still represented appropriately; never use one image that depicts a different physical form, outfit, relationship state or ending.
Review existing art to the same standard as new art.
Every assigned image requires independent review for likeness, attractive adult presentation, age appearance, anatomy, race traits, scene action, wardrobe, material behavior, and crop.
An image-generation result, a reviewer score, or a portrait alone does not complete a route.
Record explicit intimate beats as distinct scene states; use the most direct depiction that the image tool permits, and preserve an honest note if a requested explicit visual is blocked or unavailable.
Group relationships require their own reviewed art when the actual route establishes the group, including shared intimate milestones and group endings; pair art does not count as trio coverage.
